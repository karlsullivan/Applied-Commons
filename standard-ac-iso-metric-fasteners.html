ISO metric is the most common screw thread in the world, stocked by every hardware shop, so any module bolts to any frame plate with locally bought fasteners. The verification's fixes (correct part standards, current editions, a strength class) cost nothing at purchase. Corrosion and galvanic pairs are the usual way outdoor fasteners fail, and inserts are the usual fix for printed parts.

Specification

Threads: ISO metric coarse, ISO 68-1:2023 profile, sizes from ISO 262:2023, tolerance 6g/6H (ISO 965-1:2026). Sizes: M6 for frame-to-module mounting, M4 for panels and sensor brackets, M3 for small electronics and 3D-printed parts. Parts: hex nuts ISO 4032 (DIN 934); hex head screws ISO 4017:2022 and bolts ISO 4014:2022 (DIN 933/931); socket head cap screws ISO 4762 (DIN 912) where clearance is tight; flat washers ISO 7089 under heads and nuts on sheet and plastic; clearance holes per ISO 273. Parts are bought to the current editions of these standards; the yearly re-verification checks the editions. Strength: steel property class 8.8 minimum for M6 structural mounts, with class 8 nuts (ISO 898-1, ISO 898-2); 4.8 or better elsewhere. Corrosion: outdoors and in wet or animal housing, stainless A2-70 (A4-70 near the coast or in manure and biogas atmospheres) per ISO 3506-1 for screws and ISO 3506-2 for nuts. Steel M3-M6 fasteners outdoors use a zinc flake coating (ISO 10683) or electroplated zinc (ISO 4042), not hot-dip galvanising, which ISO 10684 covers only from M8 because it clogs smaller threads; hot-dip galvanised fasteners (ISO 10684) are for M8 and larger. Stainless fasteners go against aluminium or galvanised parts only with an insulating washer, since the less noble metal corrodes. Vibration (pumps, motors, fans): nyloc nuts to ISO 7040, with the steel property class from ISO 898-2 or the stainless grade from ISO 3506-2. Printed parts: load-carrying threads go into brass heat-set inserts or captive nuts, never cut into the plastic; ISO 898 classes do not apply to plastic, so load-bearing printed parts are validated by test. Grid: module mounting holes on a 100 mm grid, 6.6 mm clearance holes for M6 (ISO 273 medium); the grid is an Applied Commons design choice, not an external standard.
