Low-Power Handwashing Station

From Applied Commons, the open catalogue of ways to meet human needs
Low-Power Handwashing Station
Kindmixed
Maturitydocumented
NeedsSanitation and hygiene
This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

A contact-free or push-button handwashing station meeting the JMP 'basic hygiene' requirement (soap AND water, on-premises): a timed water pump, a soap dispenser, and a sealed control/case, powered from a small battery/battery+PV or mains. Serves a single household, a homestead, or as a shared community-facility unit (clinic/school, 50-500 people/day). The open MicroPython control and 3D-printed waterproof case are reproducible locally.
