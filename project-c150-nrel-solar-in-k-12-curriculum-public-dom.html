This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open US-federal public-domain NREL/DOE solar & renewable-energy curriculum resources (free lessons, with a free 10-lesson course covering solar electric, solar thermal, wind, micro-hydro and renewable-energy economics). Maturity: NREL national-lab, public domain, widely used. This is the best publicly-open energy-training candidate retrieved, but it is K-12/education-oriented rather than a vocational applied-technician pipeline, so it only partially meets the energy 'technician pipeline' requirement; note that commercial providers such as SEI offer more technician-focused training but are not open-licensed.
