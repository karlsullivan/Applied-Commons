This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Locally-fabricatable neonatal incubator for resource-limited hospitals: ESP32-S3 control board (PID control of heaters, fans and humidifier; medical-grade SHT4x/STS3x/SpO2 sensors; optional phototherapy; safety alarms) plus an Elecrow CrowPanel Advance 7.0 HMI display, with redundant power and WiFi/GPRS (ThingsBoard) remote telemetry. Public firmware, hardware CAD/PCB and build docs; 200+ units installed across 30+ countries by NGO Medical Open World.
