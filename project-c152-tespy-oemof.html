Open-source (MIT, 426 stars, actively maintained) Python toolkit for simulating thermal-engineering plants, including vapor-compression refrigeration, heat pumps and absorption cycles (COP, exergy, cycle sizing, fluid properties via CoolProp). It is the open design/computation layer for engineering and right-sizing off-grid mechanical cold rooms and for selecting low-GWP refrigerants -- filling the brief's 'design path, not yet a mass-distributed product' gap for the mechanical-cooling tier. It is a simulation tool, not a fieldable unit; use it to size and specify the cooling, paired with a buildable hardware design.
