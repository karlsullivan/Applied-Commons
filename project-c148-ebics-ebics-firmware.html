GPL-3.0 (license text in README; no LICENSE file) FOC (field-oriented-control) e-bike controller firmware for Lishui hub motors, with Bafang/Kunteng/Kingmeter display support, throttle/torque-simulation/torque-sensor modes and a user-friendly GUI. Mature (fielded) with ~227 stars / 392 forks; active 2026-10. Adds open pedal-assist to a cargo bike (pairs with a carrier such as FlatTrike); because it is pedal-assist, the rider can keep pedaling when a battery loses range in deep cold.
