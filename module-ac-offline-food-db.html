This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

REUSE of existing module. Offline-first mirror of an open Open Food Facts slice (barcode, product and nutrient composition) so meal planning and dietary-adequacy checks work with no internet; pairs with the existing Cauldron meal planner to make a WHO-benchmark diet checkable and affordable for low-income households. Closes the affordability / adequacy gap.
