Used by5 modules

Every geospatial module emits and consumes rasters and vectors. One set of OGC and IETF formats, readable by GDAL, QGIS and every web map, makes their outputs composable at no licence cost. COG lets field tools read just the part of a large raster they need, GeoPackage works offline, and the licence rule keeps derived data legal to share.

Specification

Rasters: Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF per the OGC Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF Standard 1.0 (OGC 21-026), with GeoTIFF 1.1 georeferencing (OGC 19-008r4); one band per variable, units and scale factor in the band metadata, nodata declared. Coordinate reference system: WGS 84 (EPSG:4326) for global or multi-region layers, a WGS 84 UTM zone or the national grid for local work, always declared in the file; tools must not assume latitude-longitude axis order. Vectors: GeoJSON per RFC 7946 for exchange, which is always WGS 84 longitude-latitude and carries no crs member; OGC GeoPackage 1.4 (OGC 12-128r19) for projected, large or multi-layer data and for offline field use. Shapefile only for importing from older tools, never as an Applied Commons output. Tiling, where a dataset is tiled: 10 x 10 degree tiles named by their north-west corner, with a footprint index in GeoJSON. Metadata in the file or a sidecar: title, source datasets and their licences, processing date (ac-iso8601-timestamps), coordinate reference system, resolution and units. Licence: layers Applied Commons makes from its own or public-domain inputs are released CC0; a layer derived from licensed data keeps that data's terms (OpenStreetMap-derived data stays under the ODbL, share-alike; satellite products keep their providers' attribution).
