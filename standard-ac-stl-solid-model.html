3MF is the open, unit- and material-aware successor to STL and is now an ISO standard (ISO/IEC 25422); STEP and ISO/ASTM 52900 are open ISO standards. Removes the prior spec's core defects (no units, no material, ambiguous orientation) while keeping STL for legacy printers.

Specification

Primary exchange container is 3MF (ISO/IEC 25422:2025), with the model unit set explicitly to mm. Conformance: every mesh of type 'model' is closed and watertight as the 3MF Core requires - manifold edges, consistently oriented triangles, outward-facing normals. The Euler characteristic may be reported as information only and is never a pass/fail gate, so parts with through-holes (washers, gaskets, brackets, gears) are valid. Material: the <basematerials> name is a portable design-intent display name for mapping to print materials; the normative FFF material type and properties, and the process settings (layer height, infill, print orientation), are carried in the optional PrintTicket part or a named vendor extension with a MustPreserve relationship. They are process hints that a consumer may override with its own defaults, not 3MF Core fields. Binary STL (80-byte header + triangles, unit mm) is retained only as a compatibility fallback and, because it carries no units or material, must have its unit declared in adjacent metadata and be re-expressed in 3MF on import. Design masters may be carried as STEP (ISO 10303-238:2022) for CAD geometry exchange only: the published Edition 3 is the machining-oriented AP, additive-manufacturing semantics are out of its scope (they are in the in-ballot Edition 4, ISO/DIS 10303-238), so 3MF remains the AM carrier. Vocabulary aligns to ISO/ASTM 52900:2021 additive-manufacturing fundamentals. The format is safety-neutral: whether a printed spare is fit for use (material, mechanical loading) is governed by the application standard for that spare.
