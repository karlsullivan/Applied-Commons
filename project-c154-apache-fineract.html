Open-source cloud-ready core banking system (Apache-2.0, 2,547 stars / 2,859 forks, actively maintained) for digital financial services incl. the unbanked/underbanked. Provides client data, loan and savings portfolio management, integrated real-time double-entry accounting, open REST APIs and on-premise or cloud deployment. Mature and documented. Fits to-harvest food credit plus mobile savings for smallholders and group/cooperative lending via open core banking; re-runnable, integrated accounting meets the idempotent-posting constraint. Caveats: deployment/configuration and local-language UX burden remain non-trivial, and savings-group/cooperative patronage accounting is not first-class.
