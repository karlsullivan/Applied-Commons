This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

A local bridge that reconciles the open field-data platforms - ODK, Kobo and the FHIR/CHT health tier - so one submission is not re-entered: entity mapping, deduplication, and queued sync over the local mesh with no internet required. Also distributes early-warning alerts (CAP 1.2) from the aggregation node to readers. Closes the 'cloud-first assumes connectivity' gap.
