Open+Pattern

From Applied Commons, the open catalogue of ways to meet human needs
Open+Pattern
NeedClothing and bodily protection
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestropical, arid, temperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source library of garment patterns digitised from real vintage/military garments, free under CC BY 4.0, provided in CLO3D/DXF/PDF/GLB/PNG. Includes cold-weather pieces (e.g. the M1951 fishtail parka, Swedish army jackets) and rain/wind coverings. Helps makers and designers reproduce well-proven, durable garments including cold-weather outerwear from documented, graded patterns. Maturity: mature and documented, with a versioned, size-graded pattern library.
