This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

A passive-solar Deep Winter Greenhouse (DWG) for northern cold climates (developed by UMN, building on Dan Handeen's Center for Sustainable Building Research work) that grows cold-hardy greens through the winter using a steep south-facing glazing wall plus underground soil/rock thermal mass, aiming for net-zero energy even through a northern winter; public farm-scale (v2.1) and hobbyist (v2.2) construction manuals, a DIY-on-a-budget report and an operating guide are published. Cold-climate food/energy candidate: year-round production in long freezing winters (Minnesota / northern US).

References

Pesch) - 8 Upper-Midwest DWG operators, winter 2017-18: avg $61/sq ft start-up (range $20-$133), avg $892/yr variable cost, 3 operators zero propane, 3 operators >=80% gross margin, 21 DWGs in region (14 commercial), sizes 288-3264 sq ft drive.google.com
  3. Flavin Hodge, Rogers, Handeen, Schweser - Yield of Leafy Greens and Microclimate in Deep Winter Greenhouse Production in Minnesota, Sustainability 2019, 11(1):28 - peer-reviewed 3-site, 12-cultivar winter yield and microclimate trial (~16 degC, 88% RH; first cutting ~76 g/gutter); DWG 'constructed of common, low-cost building and greenhouse supplies' mdpi.com
  4. Cost to Build a Greenhouse: Calculator (Sierra Greenhouse) - secondary reference citing UMN's 2018 DWG enterprise analysis at ~$33/sq ft (construction-only read; primary source says $61/sq ft all-in) sierragreenhouse.com
