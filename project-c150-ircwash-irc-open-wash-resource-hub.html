IRCwash (IRC) open WASH resource hub
NeedSkills and know-how
ServesWater, Continuity of essential services, Sanitation and hygiene
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestropical, arid, temperate
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Free, open library of WASH knowledge and training materials since 1968, including trainer's guides for village handpump mechanics (e.g. India Mark III / VLOM) and operations-and-maintenance training packages for pumps, enabling local water technicians to repair and maintain rural water points. Mature, documented, widely used in SSA and South Asia.
