Module sizes for transport and handling

From Applied Commons, the open catalogue of ways to meet human needs
Module sizes for transport and handling
Codeac-transport-dimensions
Kindmechanical
StatusApproved
ApprovedOctober 2026
Used by4 modules

Modules have to reach villages by truck, container and on foot. Sizing them to standard pallets and containers keeps shipping cheap; the weight and door limits mean they can be carried and installed without equipment; ISPM 15 stops crates being held at a border.

Specification

Modules and kits are sized to travel. A packed module fits on a 1200 x 800 mm pallet (ISO 6780; the EUR pallet of EN 13698-1) or a 1200 x 1000 mm pallet, at most 1.2 m high, so pallets stack in trucks and pack into ISO 668 freight containers. Anything one person carries weighs at most 25 kg, a common manual-handling guideline; heavier items have lifting points or handles for two people, or forklift entry. A module that must pass through an ordinary door is at most 800 mm wide. Wooden packaging for international shipment is treated and marked to ISPM 15 (heat treatment HT: at least 56 C through the core for at least 30 minutes, with the IPPC mark). The build pack states packed weight and dimensions.
