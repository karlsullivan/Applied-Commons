MOFGA — The Common Throne (composting outhouses)
NeedSanitation and hygiene
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestropical, arid, temperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Composting outhouse system documented by the Maine Organic Farmers & Gardeners Association; first throne built 2004, monitored through winter 2004-05 (pile heated; E. coli tested by Woods End Lab, within acceptable limits), reused the following fair, and units operated across fall/winter/spring workshops. Very low cost, volunteer-built, reused materials. Cold-climate, winter-documented (Maine).
