This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

On-device, no-subscription sleep and readiness/recovery computation from a consumer wearable, with a portable local record. Two documented paths: a BLE decoder that reads the band directly (derived from OpenStrap/edge) and a health-API readiness rollup from Fitbit/Google Health data (derived from Luraxx/pulse). Removes the vendor subscription and cloud lock-in (e.g. WHOOP ~EUR199/yr) that turns a purchased wearable into a paperweight; exports a local SQLite/CSV record and the standard sleep episode.
