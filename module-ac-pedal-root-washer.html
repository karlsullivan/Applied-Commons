Pedal-powered root washer (batch / throughput)

Pedal-powered root washer (batch / throughput)
Kindhardware
Maturitydocumented
NeedsFood and nutrition
LicenceCERN-OHL-S-2.0
This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

NEW. Human-pedal-powered root/produce washer: a staved barrel driven by a low-gear friction drive from a pedal / exercise bike, with a sprayer pipe admitting fine water jets; roots roll against the staves and wash water drains out through the stave gaps. Batch (~30 kg roots in ~5 min, one person) or throughput (2-3 people). No motor, no grid. Closes the clean-preparation gap (WHO Five Keys: safe handling; reduce surface pathogen load on the food) using safe water and a simple, locally repairable machine.
