This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Local, privacy-first home-automation hub that runs on a Raspberry Pi and integrates temperature/humidity sensors, thermostats, HVAC climate entities and weather feeds for whole-home climate control, scheduling and monitoring. It is the orchestration layer that ties the room climate controllers and ambient weather nodes together over the local network, with no cloud or subscription.
