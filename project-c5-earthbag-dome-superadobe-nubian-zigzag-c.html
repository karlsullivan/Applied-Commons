Earthbag Dome / Superadobe 'Nubian Zigzag' curved-wall construction
NeedShelter and thermal comfort
StatusDiscovered
LicenceNone found
This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open, illustrated build documentation of an earthbag dome structure (curved/dome load-bearing earth walls in the Nubian 'zigzag' / superadobe family). The builder's partner trained in superadobe directly with Nader Khalili at Cal-Earth; the build used locally-sourced materials and hand tools and was designed to withstand the site's climatic factors while providing an enclosed space. Demonstrates the curved, load-bearing earth-wall variant that adds passive thermal mass, wind resistance, and individually replaceable wall sections.
