Open 3D-printed orthopedic insole design pipeline (Green AI Hub / Herges)

Open 3D-printed orthopedic insole design pipeline (Green AI Hub / Herges)
Kindmixed
Maturitydocumented
NeedsClothing and bodily protection
This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Pedobarography-driven custom insole design pipeline: a participant walks on a pressure plate, ML predicts insole keypoints/parameters, a Fusion 360 plugin applies them to a base insole model, and a modified Orca Slicer exports the infill for 3D printing. Replaces foam-impression hand-crafting and subtractive EVA milling (~80% material waste) with additive printing (claimed >70% EVA material savings).
