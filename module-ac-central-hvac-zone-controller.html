This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

A Raspberry Pi controller that orchestrates up to two heating stages, two cooling stages and variable-speed dampers across up to three zones, following per-zone room-thermal-setpoints from the local climate hub. It replaces the building's OEM thermostats with an open, local, subscription-free control loop with stage-priority logic, emergency bypass and a secondary-sensor fallback per zone, and publishes zone temperatures to the MQTT bus.
