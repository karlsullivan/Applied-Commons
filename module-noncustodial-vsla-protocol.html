Programmeable non-custodial rotating-savings (VSLA / 'susu' / chit) protocol and public web clients on Stellar Soroban smart contracts: savings, loans and a rotating payout kept on-chain, non-custodial (the group self-holds the keys), auditable and offline-tolerant via the settlement node. Complements the local-ledger module with an on-chain, tamper-evident option for the 30M-member savings-group sector.
