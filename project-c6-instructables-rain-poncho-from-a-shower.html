Instructables Rain Poncho from a Shower Curtain

This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Free 4-step DIY guide (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, ~32k views) to cut and sew a waterproof hooded rain poncho from a recycled shower curtain, with vinyl-sewing tips. Helps monsoon/low-resource makers who need cheap, simple rain protection. Maturity: free, published and long-standing (2008); the body fabric is a reused consumer product rather than a locally-sourced textile, but the construction is simple and low-cost.
