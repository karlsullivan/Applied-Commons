This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

A self-contained electric powertrain (hub or in-hub motor, controller, 24/48 V BMS) for an L-category vehicle (L1e-L7e), grounded in OpenEV (GPL-2.0, 'complete Electric Vehicle', KiCad hardware + C/C++ + Python). Provides propulsion for the e-assist cargo-bike variant and for an L7e light vehicle in village, homestead and small-farm scenarios; the battery is the shared swap-bay pack so the same pack also powers the off-grid cold-chain box.
