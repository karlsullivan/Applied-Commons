IncuNest

From Applied Commons, the open catalogue of ways to meet human needs
IncuNest
NeedReproductive and early-life needs
StatusScreened
LicenceMIT
Sourcegithub.com
Last updated upstreamOctober 2026
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Low-cost (~350 EUR) open-source neonatal incubator by NGO Medical Open World; public ESP32 firmware, hardware CAD/PCB and build docs; 200+ units installed across 30+ countries. Mature, deployed, locally buildable; open for non-commercial use.
