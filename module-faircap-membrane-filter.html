This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open humanitarian membrane filter (0.1 / 0.01 micron) that screws onto jerrycans, buckets or drums and removes ~99.99% of bacteria plus protozoa and cysts without energy or chemicals; low-cost, durable, no-grid, low-maintenance household drinking-water enabler. Strong for the WASH slice of the brief; membrane is imported (China), so it does not meet a strict local-sourcing constraint by itself.
