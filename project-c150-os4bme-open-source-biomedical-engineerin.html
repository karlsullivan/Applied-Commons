OS4BME (Open-Source Biomedical Engineering for Africa)
NeedSkills and know-how
ServesPhysical and mental health, Reproductive and early-life needs, Continuity of essential services
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestropical, arid
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source biomedical device project using a DIY & makers approach — building a solar-powered neonatal baby monitor on the open-source iRobota electronics platform with 3D printing, component selection, assembly and testing — supported by a consortium of nine African universities with UNECA, and shared openly via blog and WIKI. Maturity: active multi-university consortium. Meets the health requirement for open device designs plus hands-on installation/maintenance/user know-how for LMIC settings.
