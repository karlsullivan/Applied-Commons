This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Grid-free, wireless water-storage tank monitor: measures level (0-100%) and estimated volume (liters) with a waterproof ultrasonic sensor, reports battery state, and raises low/full alerts; installs on 100-2000 L tanks at households, homesteads, small farms and village facilities; optional Home Assistant telemetry (local only, no internet).
