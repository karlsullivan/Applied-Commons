This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open, reproducible design-and-simulation record for a solar-powered NH3-H2O continuous absorption refrigerator for off-grid, dry/hot climates (Sudan-targeted): a 200 W / 150 L cold box with a CPC solar collector, model COP 0.424, public OpenSCAD/CadQuery CAD, a P&ID and a CoolProp/teqp thermodynamic model, plus a public BOM. Serves the mechanical-cooling design path for small-scale R1 in arid climates. Carried as a design reference, not a deployable product.
