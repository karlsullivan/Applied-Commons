Open-source modular cargo-bike and cargo-trike system by the N55 art collective (Copenhagen, 2011) with Till Wolfer, released under Creative Commons. It uses a bolted, no-weld orthogonal aluminium-tube spaceframe with standard off-the-shelf bicycle parts (no dedicated mechanic or welding needed), is designed for DIY build/repair/modification and local fair production, and has functional add-on modules (food, medical, mobile-lab, power). COLD-CLIMATE CANDIDATE: designed, handbuilt and ridden daily in Copenhagen/Denmark and Hamburg/Germany, long-freezing-winter regions (selection basis is design/origin geography, not an independent extreme-cold field test). Fits the low-cost, locally buildable, repairable goods-carrier requirement well, though it is human-powered rather than rated for 40-80 km/day heavy loads on rural tracks.
