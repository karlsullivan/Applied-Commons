This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open irrigation control for food production: OpenSprinkler is a mature, widely deployed open irrigation controller (GPL-3.0 firmware, 500+ stars, active 2026, with hardware designs) that runs pumps/valves from schedules and sensors, and sploosh (GPL-3.0) is a simple open PID controller that reads a capacitive soil-moisture sensor and switches a small water pump only at the target moisture. Together they cover the soil-moisture-driven irrigation requirement with locally buildable, low-cost, open parts.
