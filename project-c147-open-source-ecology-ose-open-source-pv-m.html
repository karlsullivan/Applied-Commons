This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open, documented, low-cost solar-panel assembly process (Open Source Ecology wiki) that assembles purchased PV cells into new modules: a solar-powered semi-automated line with roughly US$150k-500k total startup cost for about 2 MW/year, and up to 15 MW/year at scale. Provides the open, locally buildable energy-manufacturing (panel assembly and repair) process the brief asks for, turning cells into repairable local panels rather than bought, closed ones.
