This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open build (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, ~US$24 per panel) for DIY acoustic wall/ceiling panels from wood, rigid fiberglass/acoustic filler, and fabric that absorb sound and cut reverberant/echo noise in a room. Helps sleepers in shared or thin-walled bedrooms near traffic or noisy neighbours. Passive, no grid power, buildable with a saw and basic fasteners. Mature: published with a full materials list, panel dimensions, and step-by-step instructions. Note: reduces reverberation rather than serving as a quantified STC barrier to external air-borne noise.
