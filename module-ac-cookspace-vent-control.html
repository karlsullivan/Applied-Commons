EXTENSION of the existing ac-cookspace-vent-control (same code, not a duplicate). Keeps its existing role - closing the loop over the source-side hardware by reading the PM2.5 sensor and driving the exhaust fan/valve - and adds a CO safety interlock variant: it consumes the new ac-cookspace-combustion-gas-sensor over the MQTT bus; when CO is above threshold it forces the exhaust fan (ac-hearth-fan-exhaust) to full via its dry-contact relay and drives the local alarm, and it applies a fail-safe that turns the fan ON if the sensor signal is lost or the fan is not confirmed running. This is the control half of the CO safety chain and makes the gas sensor's relay meaningful.
