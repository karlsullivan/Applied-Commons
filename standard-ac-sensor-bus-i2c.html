The Qwiic / STEMMA QT connector is the cheapest, most widely available plug-and-play sensor system, with hundreds of compatible boards from several makers, so modules can be assembled without soldering. Keeping I2C inside the enclosure respects its electrical limits.

Specification

Sensors and small boards inside one enclosure connect by I2C (NXP UM10204) on 4-pin JST SH 1.0 mm connectors in the Qwiic / STEMMA QT layout: GND, 3.3 V, SDA, SCL (black, red, blue, yellow wires), 3.3 V logic only. Standard mode (100 kbit/s) by default; fast mode (400 kbit/s) where the bus is short. Total bus capacitance at most 400 pF, which in practice keeps cable runs well under 1 m inside an enclosure. Each device's 7-bit address is listed in its documentation, and no two devices on one bus share an address (otherwise use an I2C multiplexer). Pull-up resistors are fitted once per bus (typically 2.2 to 4.7 kohm to 3.3 V). I2C never leaves the enclosure: field sensors use ac-4-20ma-field-signal, RS-485 (ac-modbus-control) or a radio standard.

Used by
