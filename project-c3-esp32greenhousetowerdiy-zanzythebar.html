Open-source (MIT) DIY ESP32-based automated aeroponic or hydroponic modular tower garden: an interlocking 3D-printable vertical tower that adapts to a common 20L/5-gallon bucket reservoir, with an optional 3D-printed aeroponic nozzle. Arduino-C++ firmware with MQTT/Home Assistant integration and a web configurator; base parts under ~$20, a full ~1 m tower around $100 plus ~$40 of PCBs. Beginner-friendly and cheap enough for low-budget builds; indoor (not itself rated for freezing). Maturity: established hobby project - ~227 commits, active since 2021, well documented.
