A publicly available open design manual for the household biosand filter, the widely deployed low-cost slow-sand point-of-use filter developed by Dr. David Manz (University of Calgary) and scaled by CAWST. It explains how the filter makes water safe, the role of each component (sand bed, schmutzdecke/biolayer, gravel, container), what water can be treated, and operation including the pause/charge period. Built from simple, locally available materials (concrete or plastic container, local sand) at household, school, or small-community scale, with no grid power and maintenance within local capacity. It is the most-deployed open household treatment design and gives strong turbidity and E. coli removal.
