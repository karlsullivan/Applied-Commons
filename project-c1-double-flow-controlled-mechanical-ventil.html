This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Well-documented DIY heat recovery ventilator (double-flow, counter-flow) built from ductwork, fans and a heat-exchanger core, published 2017 under CC BY-NC-SA 35k+ views, featured project. Gives cold-climate homes fresh-air ventilation without losing heating - the standard passive/low-power answer for freezing winters where simple ventilation alone would not reach guideline levels. Buildable with common hand tools and store-bought ducting.
