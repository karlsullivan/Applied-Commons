This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

A public 28-step build guide (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) for a multipurpose direct solar pumping system that transfers water from a creek, dam or bore to storage, irrigates ~5 ha and supplies drinking water to stock - i.e., water-to-premises plus dry-season irrigation, using a direct-coupled (minimal-storage) solar design that matches the 'minimize battery storage' constraint. DIY, low-cost and common-tool buildable; the non-commercial clause of the CC BY-NC-SA license is the one caveat for commercial reuse.
