This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open Python (IDAES) framework for sizing and simulating reverse-osmosis and desalination/dessalination flowsheets (membrane selection, recovery, energy, reject handling); used to design a community RO plant or to evaluate whether a scarce/saline source is worth treating, and to size the upstream pretreatment that the multi-barrier drinking-water module feeds.
