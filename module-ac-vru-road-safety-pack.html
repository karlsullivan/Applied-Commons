This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Add-on safety pack for the cargo-trike / LSEV platform: ultrasonic proximity sensing, audible and visual warning to the vehicle operator and to the pedestrian/cyclist/moto (VRU), and regenerative-brake assist. Targets >=20% reduction in VRU fatality rate over 3 years in the service area, contributing to the WHO halve-by-2030 road-safety goal.
