An advanced continuous-feed, forced-draft biomass cookstove developed by DBFZ (Leipzig, Germany) with representatives of the West African region and users in Togo, published open access and tested per ISO 19867-1:2018. It is fuel-flexible (wood, bamboo and straw pellets, palm-kernel shells), runs on a small electric fan (e.g. a solar power bank), and achieves Tier 4 low PM2.5 emissions. It serves the cooking requirement (indoor air quality toward the WHO household-fuel-combustion target) and the health need, is designed to be affordable and fuel-fitted to West African contexts, and the open-access design documentation supports local replication.
