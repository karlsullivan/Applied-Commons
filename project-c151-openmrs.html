This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source (Apache-2.0) electronic medical records system 'built by a global community' - no license fees or vendor lock-in, highly translatable, with REST and FHIR APIs. Used in 70+ countries across 8,000+ facilities with 15 million patient records, adaptable for primary care, NCD, maternal/child and infectious-disease programs in local languages. Maturity: very high (openmrs/openmrs-core, 1,940 stars, active).
