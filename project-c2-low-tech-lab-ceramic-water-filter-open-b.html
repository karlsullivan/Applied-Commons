This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

A complete open-wiki, step-by-step build guide (15 steps) for producing a ceramic pot water filter at semi-industrial scale using local clay mixed with a combustible material (sawdust/rice husks), pressed, fired, flow-tested, and treated with colloidal silver. Published by Low-tech Lab with cost (~20 EUR per filter), duration (~3 days), and case studies, in multiple languages. Gravity-fed, no grid power, locally sourceable materials, and operable/maintainable at community level; the fired ceramic provides physical entrapment/adsorption and colloidal silver adds antibacterial action. It is a low-cost, no-power treatment design that works across climates including low-sun regions (unlike solar disinfection).
