A portable, off-grid room / breathing-zone PM2.5 purifier for the living/cooking space that does NOT depend on an imported disposable HEPA/MERV cartridge. A cheap 5 V USB fan draws air through a frame of layered, locally available washable cloth/fabric media (multi-layer t-shirt / muslin / local woven cloth, optionally with a fine mesh pre-stage) that captures a large fraction of cooking PM2.5. The cloth is reusable - washed and re-tensioned locally - so there is no continuing imported consumable supply chain, the exact gap a disposable-filter purifier cannot close. A 3D-printed or locally made box, a USB fan and fabric are all household-buildable and well under US$100. This is the room/breathing-zone layer that complements the source-side hood.
