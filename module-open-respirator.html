Open 3D-printed particulate respirator (NASA JPL lineage)
Kindhardware
Maturitydocumented
NeedsClothing and bodily protection
This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Self-contained 3D-printed half-face respirator that mimics commercial 3M-style seal geometry with a swappable filter front (COTS 3M-style P100 or a custom pleated HEPA/N95-media cartridge). For particulate/airborne protection where commercial respirator supply chains are stressed. Purposefully uses hardware-store / hobby-shop materials rather than the medical supply chain.
