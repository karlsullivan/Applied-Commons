StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatescold, polar
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Public research report (Alaska Housing Finance Corp-funded, 2013) on passive cold-climate refrigeration: permafrost/ice cellars, winter-charged ice boxes, and thermosyphon-retrofitted refrigerators that passively hold compartment temperatures at 0 F and colder, with a ~$646 prototype BOM and measured 16% annual energy savings; also cites a UAF study on preserving thawing Arctic ice cellars. The unambiguous cold-climate candidate - long freezing winters, low winter sun, northern US / Alaska. A design/research report, not a turnkey product.
