This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Self-hosted platform to visually design, localize and scale two-way interactive flows over SMS, IVR/voice and community reporting for basic feature phones; used for life-saving early warning, market-price/advisory to smallholders, and health outreach. Offline-friendly: SMS store-and-forward when a device is out of range.
