This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

A heavy-duty, public-domain, VLOM-compliant hand pump serving ~300 people at 10-45 m lift, with a fully documented design and a local spare-parts supply chain (area mechanic -> local retailer -> local wholesaler) so a failed water point is restored locally instead of waiting on external donors.

Variants

  • Depth kits 21 / 30 / 45 m (borehole dia 100-150 mm; 0.4125 L/stroke at 30 m per IFRC)
  • Stainless (AISI 304/316) pumprods for corrosive water (pH>6.5 / iron>1 ppm) vs galvanised mild-steel rods (cheaper where water is non-corrosive)
