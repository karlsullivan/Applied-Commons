This module is non-commercial: it is based on Prusa Face Shield, whose licence does not allow commercial use, and keeps that licence (CC-BY-NC). It may be copied and changed, but not sold or used commercially, and neither may anything made from it.

Outdoor-worker kit matching the WHO guidance for UV index >=3: ventilated UPF>=40 (50 preferred) full-coverage garment, broad-brimmed hat covering eyes/face/ears/neck, and wraparound eyewear blocking 99-100% UVA/UVB; breathable moisture-wicking construction so evaporative cooling is not blocked (heat-stress mechanism from the need_brief).
