This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

AGPLv3 open-source energy management system for photovoltaics/solar, batteries/energy storage, EV charging and heat pumps. It is the open control and coordination layer that keeps off-grid and mini-grid power available and serviceable without proprietary OEM controllers. Mature and active (created 2016, ~1.6k GitHub stars, regular development).
