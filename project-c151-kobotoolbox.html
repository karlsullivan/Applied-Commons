This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source (KoboForm/KoboToolbox, AGPL-3.0) data-collection, management and visualization platform used by 32,000+ organizations across 220+ countries, purpose-built for low-resource and challenging field settings with fully user-owned data and access control. Runs offline in the field and self-hosts data in-country, meeting the local data-sovereignty and maintainability constraints. Maturity: very high and very actively maintained.
