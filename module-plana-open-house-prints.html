This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open, free-to-download architectural drawing set for a 42 m2 two-bedroom pitched-roof timber frame engineered to UK and Irish construction regulations on a modular grid, on screw piles or simple pad foundations, using off-the-shelf hardware-store materials with cladding left open to local climate/availability/budget. A commons seed for formal, regulated, cold-climate housing contexts; NOT a ready-to-build approval package - requires licensed architect/engineer review.
