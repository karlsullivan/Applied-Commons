The need

About 3.45 billion people (43% of the world) do not use mobile internet, overwhelmingly in low- and middle-income countries. In low-income countries only 27% are online vs 93% in high-income countries, and roughly one-third of humanity (concentrated in rural areas) is offline. 119 of ~196 countries (60%) have a multi-hazard early warning system, so the remaining ~40% (77 countries, incl. most SIDS at 43% and the low-scoring African region) are not covered. Sub-Saharan Africa is the least-connected region (~13% coverage gap, ~60% usage gap).

Severe and persistent. Early warning systems give a ~10x return on investment yet 40% of countries lack one and the UN 'everyone protected by 2027' goal is off track. Hazards are intensifying: the planet is ~1.44C warmer than pre-industrial and 2015-2025 were the 11 warmest years on record. The >3-billion usage gap persists into 2025 despite 200 million more connecting in 2024. The stakes are large: connecting the unconnected adds ~US$3.5 trillion to global GDP over 2023-2030, 90% of it in LMICs. Africa's early-warning comprehensiveness rose 72% since 2015 but it is still the lowest-scoring region.

What people do now

Offline / low-connectivity communities rely on broadcast and community channels - radio, television, sirens, SMS to basic feature phones, and locally-led feedback - because mobile data is out of reach. Broadband is unaffordable for most of the target population: only 62 of 134 low- and middle-income economies meet the Broadband Commission target of entry-level service below 2% of monthly GNI per capita; the global median mobile broadband basket (2GB/month) is 1.3% of GNI per capita and fixed broadband 2.9%. Fixed broadband where it exists is ~2-3x the mobile price, so the mobile / feature-phone route is the only viable path for most.

Where existing solutions fall short

43% of the population still does not use mobile internet and the >3-billion usage gap persists into 2025. ~40% of countries (77) have no multi-hazard early warning system; SIDS are at 43% and Africa is the lowest-scoring region. Only 62 of 134 LMICs meet the affordability target and fixed broadband remains a 2-3x mobile-price luxury; the urban-rural divide is not closing. Existing systems are not equipped for the emerging, intensifying hazards that will drive future loss.

What a solution must do

  • Safety/life: deliver a life-saving early warning to 100% of people at risk via multi-channel (cell broadcast + SMS + radio + siren), including to basic feature phones with no smartphone or data.
  • Safety/life: warning in the local language, timely and actionable, reaching the last mile offline or on minimal data.
  • Food/income: carry market-price and advisory information to smallholders over SMS/IVR on basic phones; documented effect of digital advisory + market linkages is yield +23-73% and income +18-37%.
  • Health: support remote clinical consultation (telemedicine) to extend essential health services to rural and underserved facilities where distance is the barrier.
  • All needs/affordability: keep entry-level data at or below 2% of monthly GNI per capita (the Broadband Commission target) so the poorest can actually use it.
  • Water/sanitation: carry early warning for water-related hazards - floods, drought and emerging hazards such as glacial lake outburst floods - that current systems do not yet handle.

Constraints

  • Last-mile connectivity and power are weak/unreliable where these people live; the system must operate where commercial backhaul does not.
  • Must work on feature phones and offline (SMS stores and delivers when the device is back in range); smartphone+app dependence disqualifies a solution.
  • Affordability ceiling: most LMICs are above the 2% GNI target and real mobile data usage (~11GB/user in 2021, forecast >3x by 2027) far exceeds the 2GB reference basket, so cost must be engineered down.
  • Localization is mandatory: messages must be understandable and co-developed with local communities to be trusted and acted on.
  • Must be extensible to emerging intensifying hazards (extreme heat, wildfires, glacial lake outburst floods) beyond current system scope.
