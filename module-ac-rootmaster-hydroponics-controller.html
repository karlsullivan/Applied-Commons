This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

REUSE of existing module. Open hydroponic automation controller (STM32G4 board + Raspberry-Pi-class host running the open hypo CLI) for pH, EC, water/air temperature sensing and pump/valve control of nutrient solution in soilless production (household to small farm). Closes the clean-production / production-inputs gap.
