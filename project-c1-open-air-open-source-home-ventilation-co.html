Open AIR (open-source home ventilation control)
NeedAir and breathing
StatusScreened
LicenceGPL-3.0
Sourcegithub.com
Last updated upstreamFebruary 2026
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source controllers, valve and sensors that retrofit off-the-shelf ventilation units (e.g. Duco boxes, Orcon) for duct CO2/humidity/temperature monitoring and 0-100% valve control, all driven through Home Assistant. GPL-3.0, maintained (~102 stars, last push 2026-02).
