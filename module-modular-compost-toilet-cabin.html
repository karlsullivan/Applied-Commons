Modular Pallet-Wood Composting Toilet Cabin

From Applied Commons, the open catalogue of ways to meet human needs
Modular Pallet-Wood Composting Toilet Cabin
Kindhardware
Maturitydocumented
NeedsSanitation and hygiene
This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

A weather-resistant, tool-light, modular wooden cabin (front, back, 2x side, centre insert, floor plate, sit plate, roof) that houses a urine-diverting dry/composting toilet; side panels are shared between adjacent units so a row of N units saves a side panel per joint. Reclaimed-pallet construction keeps it buildable and low-cost in low-income settings; the roof is transparent PE corrugated for daylight. Scales from a single household unit to a shared community facility (50-500 people/day).
