Advanced continuous-feed, forced-draft biomass cookstove meeting WHO Guidelines for Indoor Air Quality (PM2.5 and CO emission targets) at ISO 19867 Tier-4 performance; fuel-flexible on wood, bamboo, straw pellets and palm-kernel shells, with a small DC fan for forced draft.
