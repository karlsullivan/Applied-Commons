Advanced continuous-feed, forced-draft biomass cookstove per the Apeli/DBFZ design (Togo user trials, ISO 19867-1:2018 tested, Tier-4 PM2.5 performance): fuel-flexible on wood, bamboo and straw pellets and palm-kernel shells, with a small DC fan powered from solar USB - closing the gap between open-fire PM levels (up to 100x acceptable) and WHO indoor targets at a price West African users can pay.
