This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Offline-first, phone-first data collection for low-literacy, connectivity-poor settings: village/WASH point surveys, disease/health enumerations and post-disaster damage collection on ordinary Android phones, with location, photos and skip-logic forms that sync when connectivity returns. ODK Collect (Apache-2.0) plus a self-hosted ODK Central (Docker Compose, 1-2 GB RAM) keep data user-owned in open export formats (CSV/Excel/GPKG). De-facto standard: 2M+ users, 250M+ submissions/yr; WHO (48 African governments, 500M+ data points) and HOT field use documented. Does not itself produce basemaps or OSM geometry; pairs with the editing station module.
