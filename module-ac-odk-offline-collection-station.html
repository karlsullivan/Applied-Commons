Offline data-collection + fleet/dispatch logging station built on ODK (Apache-2.0): ODK Collect on ordinary Android phones records check-ins, manifests, stops and geotagged photos for last-mile and community delivery, synced to a self-hosted ODK Central server. Extends the existing module with a fleet-logging variant.
