This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

A head-mounted breathing-zone device that draws air through a reusable filter with a small DC blower and feeds the facepiece under slight positive pressure. It removes the three pain points the need brief attributes to passive masks - facial fit, long-session tolerance, and a steady disposable-mask supply - and works off-grid (small rechargeable battery, USB-C). It is the powered complement to the existing passive open-respirator (NASA JPL lineage), not a duplicate: where the passive respirator protects a short task, the PAPR is for the cook standing at the hearth for the whole cooking session, and for high-pollution-city smog/bushfire days. Shared by all scenarios as personal breathing-zone protection; reusable filter = no imported daily consumable.
