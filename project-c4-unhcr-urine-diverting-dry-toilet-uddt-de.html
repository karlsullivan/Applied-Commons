UNHCR — Urine Diverting Dry Toilet (UDDT) Design and BoQ

UNHCR — Urine Diverting Dry Toilet (UDDT) Design and BoQ
NeedSanitation and hygiene
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatesarid, temperate
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

UNHCR public design and bill-of-quantities specification for a source-separating urine-diverting dry toilet with on-site treatment; detailed construction templates (urine diversion well, foot rests, chamber heights) and BoQ make it directly locally buildable for water-scarce and off-grid/refugee/emergency settings. Institutional, published, low-cost and service-chain-independent.

References

  1. Angenent et al. 2019, Frontiers in Environmental Science - Field-Scale Co-fermentation of UDDT Solid Waste (Nairobi, Kenya), Ascaris inactivation frontiersin.org
  2. Brown et al. 2024, Science of The Total Environment - Environmental evaluation of UDDTs in Hiloweyn Camp, Dollo Ado, Ethiopia (E. coli + Ascaris inactivation) sciencedirect.com
  3. UNHCR D406 UDDT Design and BoQ (landing page: .dwg, BOQ.xlsx, PDF) unhcr.org
  4. UNHCR Urine Diversion Dry Toilets Standard Operating Procedure (OG401-2018A) unhcr.org
  5. UNHCR D406 UDDT Bill of Quantities (costed: USD 657/toilet; includes 100L handwashing reservoir, no soap) unhcr.org
