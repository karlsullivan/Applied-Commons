This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Small off-grid solar-powered cold room built per the Efficiency for Access low-carbon blueprint, using natural, locally available insulation - reported to cut embedded GHG by ~63% and cost by ~20% versus polyurethane-panel cold rooms. Serves the food-preservation requirement (smallholder harvest / market node so perishables survive farm-to-market time) and, in the vaccine variant with a WHO-prequalified unit, the health-facility refrigeration requirement.

Variants

  • Food cold room: chilled to <= +5 C, ~2-3 m3, smallholder/market-node scale
  • Vaccine/medicine variant: +2 to +8 C, hosts a WHO PQS E003 prequalified unit (the AC design does not replace a prequalified one until evaluated against E003), E006-class temperature monitoring
  • Frozen variant: <= -18 C, includes sub-zero-ambient build for cold climates
