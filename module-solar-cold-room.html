This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Off-grid solar-powered refrigerated cold room with battery buffering for homestead, small-farm and village scenarios; natural local-material insulation keeps it buildable without imported components and preserves a smallholder harvest or market surplus. The central mechanical tier of the cold chain that the evaporative tiers feed into.
