This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

An open-source Turgo micro-hydro turbine design package (engineering drawings, a design spreadsheet, and 3D CAD) delivered through a practical course aimed at local manufacturers with small-hydro (<1 MW) experience in the global South; the Turgo is the classic low-cost, easily built small-hydro option that stands alone off-grid and produces year-round independent of winter sun. Backed by the University of Bristol, PEEDA, Nepal Yantra Shala Energy, Kathmandu University and the Hydro Empowerment Network.
