This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open, locally-buildable neighborhood/indoor air node measuring PM2.5/PM10, TVOC/NOx and T/RH, closing the gap between the ~6000 monitored cities and the rest of the 99% exposed. Runs on a small off-grid (USB/solar+battery) power budget and streams locally.
