Open, locally-buildable ambient/indoor air-quality node measuring PM2.5/PM10, CO2, TVOC/NOx and T/RH on a small off-grid (USB / solar+battery) budget, closing the gap between the ~6000 monitored cities and the rest of the 99% of people exposed.
