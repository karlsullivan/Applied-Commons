This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open-source mobile field-GIS app (GPL-2.0-or-later, by OPENGIS.ch; Digital Public Good since 2024) running QGIS projects fully offline in the field with high-precision built-in or external GNSS, local storage synced via cable or QFieldCloud. QField Rapid Mapper layers open, customizable disaster damage-assessment forms for emergency responders, civil-protection teams and citizens, publishing results to the web. Meets the brief's offline, phone-first, no-specialist-GIS-staff requirement; cross-platform (Android/iOS/Windows/macOS/Linux).
