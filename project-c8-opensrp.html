This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source (Apache 2.0), FHIR-native smart-register platform used by community health workers for antenatal (ANC), postnatal (PNC), child health, immunizations and family planning in low-resource settings; offline-capable, national deployments. Mature, deployed.
