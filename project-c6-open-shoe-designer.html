Open Shoe Designer

StatusScreened

Climatestropical, arid, temperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source (GPLv3) parametric shoemaking CAD that separates design, form and measurements and produces the last, the sole and the flattened upper pattern to be cut, sewn or glued. Helps people who want reproducible, custom last-based footwear patterns from open code. Maturity: pre-alpha (software is early), but the open approach and its output (cuttable upper/sole patterns) are a useful pattern-generation reference.
