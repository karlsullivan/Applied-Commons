Efficiency for Access - Designing for Sustainability: Blueprint for a Low-Carbon Cold Room

This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

A public, downloadable blueprint (full PDF report, one-page summary, and a life-cycle-assessment Excel tool) by Efficiency for Access (led by Energy Saving Trust, with UK-aid and IKEA Foundation support, and Kenya fieldwork) for building an off-grid solar-powered cold room using natural, locally available insulation materials - reported to cut embedded GHG by ~63% and cost by ~20% versus best-in-class polyurethane-panel cold rooms. Directly serves the food-preservation requirement (a smallholder harvest or market-node cold room so perishables survive farm-to-market time) and, with PV + battery, the vaccine/medicine refrigeration component of health-facility power; the locally sourced materials and open design support local build and maintainability.
