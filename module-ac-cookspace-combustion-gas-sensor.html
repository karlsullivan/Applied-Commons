A small, off-grid, hand-assemblable gas sensor for any kitchen that burns solid fuel or kerosene. It detects carbon monoxide (lethal; produced by incomplete combustion, and the main risk when a capture hood is under-powered or the stove idles) and carbon dioxide (stale-air / combustion indicator). Safety works WITHOUT connectivity: a local audible + visual alarm fires at threshold and the reading drives a relay output for fan interlock; it additionally publishes to the local MQTT bus so ac-cookspace-vent-control can act on it. It is the missing companion to ac-hearth-pm25-sensor (PM-only) and to the 'optional CO/CO2 indication' the vent controller already expects. Shared by every household, homestead, village, small-farm and community-facility cooking space.
