ServesShelter and thermal comfort, Continuity of essential services, Physical and mental health
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestemperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open US-public-domain Whole Building Design Guide resource on woody-biomass facility heating: system component breakdown, operation & maintenance (a few minutes of daily attention plus a few hours per year for annual maintenance; ~2–5 hrs/week during the heating season), and O&M cost considerations. Maturity: maintained by NIBS (reviewed/updated May 2026). Heating know-how that works year-round in cold-climate heating season; addresses the energy requirement for maintenance/repair knowledge of thermal assets.
