Open-hardware 16S (48 V nominal) Li-ion battery management system: per-cell voltage/temperature monitoring, charge/discharge over- and under-voltage and over-current protection, active/passive balancing, and status display for off-grid battery banks; locally serviceable, extendable bank capacity by parallel strings.
