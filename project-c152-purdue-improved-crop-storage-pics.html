Purdue Improved Crop Storage (PICS)
NeedCold chain and storage
StatusDiscovered
LicenceNone found
This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Hermetic grain storage: a cost-effective triple-layer bag (two HDPE inner layers + woven polypropylene outer) that stores shelled grain (maize, sorghum, wheat, rice, etc.) 6-12 months without fumigation, at ~$2.50 per 50 kg. Gates-funded PICS3 is field-deployed across West and East Africa; public Purdue extension PDFs document the design. Field-proven, low-cost, locally sourced - the direct match to the airtight/dry grain requirement. Requires shelled grain and closing labour, a known adoption constraint the brief notes.
