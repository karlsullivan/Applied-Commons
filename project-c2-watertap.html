This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source Python (IDAES) framework for designing and simulating water treatment and desalination processes including reverse osmosis; developed under the National Alliance for Water Innovation with U.S. DOE support, full docs, graphical flowsheet UI and binder tutorials; mature and institutionally backed.
