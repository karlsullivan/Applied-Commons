This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

A low-cost, open-source vertical-axis wind turbine producing roughly 150-200 W, published by Applied Sciences under a Creative Commons license (CC BY-SA 4.0) with a complete construction guide, full materials list and recommended tools, buildable from household and local-hardware-store parts (stove pipe, metal brackets, plastic sheet, a trailer hub). The public guide states it was commissioned by a Washington (USA, northern) resident 'to provide supplemental water heating' - a documented cold-climate, winter use case - and its vertical-axis design runs year-round. It is deliberately low-cost and locally maintainable with common tools, matching the brief's O&M and affordability constraints, and contributes off-grid generation for lighting, phone charging and small pumps in remote, non-grid settlements.
