This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Source-side capture: a sheet-metal / local-clay / wire-mesh hood and straight chimney duct fitted over the existing open fire or solid-fuel stove, pulling combustion smoke out at the hearth instead of letting it fill the room. It requires no clean fuel, adds no kerosene/unprocessed coal and introduces no new combustion by-products - it only removes what the existing stove already emits. Hand-tool buildable (tin snips, hammer, file), ducted out through the roof, sized to hold capture velocity at the opening. It is the vented path that ac-clean-cooking references ('a design that cannot meet the unvented rates is installed with a chimney or hood and meets the vented rates') but does not itself specify.
