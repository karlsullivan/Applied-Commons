This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source collaborative platform (Django, Azavea/Urban Ecos; 194 stars) for crowdsourced urban tree inventory, ecosystem-services calculation and community engagement. Directly targets the brief's restoration-monitoring gap by making tree survival and stand data published and verifiable rather than assumed; low-cost software a local government or community can self-host. Works year-round in any climate.
