Unit of saledozen
EvidencePotential, not proven
Community priorityEarly candidate subject to demand

A potential enterprise, not a promise of sales or profit. Requirements are a non-exhaustive candidate configuration; prices, demand, labour and costs must come from the actual place and time.

Table egg sales: selling Table eggs to Households, Farm-gate buyers, Retailers or food businesses where requirements are met.

Do not add an enterprise unless labour, buyer demand, working capital, competence and maintenance are credible; community service value may justify a separate subsidy decision.
