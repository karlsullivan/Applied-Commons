This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Self-contained open vertical-axis wind turbine that converts wind into DC for the 48 V bus, complementing PV in windy / low-sun seasons and in the dry-season demand peak. A VAWT is omni-directional (no yaw), simple to maintain and well suited to the limited local O&M capacity called out in the need brief. Shared generation block for the homestead, village and community-facility scenarios. Reuses the existing corpus code open-vawt.
