This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Community self-built line of open machine designs - shredder, granulator, twin-screw extruder, injection molder and sheet press - that turn segregated post-consumer plastic waste into pellets, sheets and molded products (filter housings, building fixings, pipes) with no municipal collection, recycling or landfill infrastructure; creates local micro-recycling livelihoods. Core circularity hub whose outputs feed the water-filter and timber modules.
