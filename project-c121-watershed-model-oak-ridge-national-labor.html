Open-source Python (Tkinter GUI) model from the Standard Modular Hydropower team at Oak Ridge National Laboratory for rapidly designing, simulating and evaluating low-head, in-stream, run-of-river micro-hydro projects. Run-of-river hydro delivers power year-round regardless of low winter sun, a key advantage in cold climates, so the model is a useful open enabler for small communities to stand up winter-reliable generation where the grid will not reach for a decade or more. Public and documented with a user guide and case studies; early-maturity (2022).
