Earthbag Building (EarthbagBuilding.com, Owen Geiger)
NeedShelter and thermal comfort
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestropical, arid, temperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Free, extensively documented system for load-bearing walls from bags of locally sourced earth, sand or scoria, stacked in courses, tied with barbed wire, then plastered. Hand-tool buildable, very low material cost, no grid needed, and adaptable to cold climates by using an insulated fill material (per the site's dedicated cold-climate article). Directly matches the low-cost, locally-buildable, no-electricity constraints in the need brief.
