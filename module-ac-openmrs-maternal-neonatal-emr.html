Java-based open-source electronic medical record used in LMIC hospitals, hosting maternal and neonatal care modules. Active since 2012, widely deployed, DPG-verified and Apache-2.0. Serves as the facility/hospital-tier record that complements OpenSRP at the community tier: the CHW smart-register captures at the field and OpenMRS is the source-of-truth clinical record in the facility, linked by FHIR/HL7.
