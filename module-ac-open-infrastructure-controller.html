This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Vendor-agnostic, locally-hosted open control/automation layer that reads node telemetry and drives pumps, irrigation valves, treatment plants and microgrid inverters via Modbus, with rules and fail-safe protection, on commodity hardware - no proprietary cloud.
