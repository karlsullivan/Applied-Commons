Vendor-agnostic, locally-hosted open control layer (openHAB, EPL-2.0) that drives pumps, irrigation valves, treatment plants and microgrid inverters via Modbus + MQTT, with rules and fail-safe protection, on commodity hardware - no proprietary cloud.
