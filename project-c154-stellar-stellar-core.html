This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source layer-1 decentralized network for fast, low-cost cross-border payments, remittances and financial inclusion (3,305 stars on stellar-core, active since 2014). Near-instant, minimal-fee settlement of digital representations of fiat/stablecoins with 180+ country reach. Fits the coping/safety requirement of remittance and wage-transfer channels well under the ~6% baseline and low-cost clinic/pharmacy payment rails. Caveat: an online blockchain network, not offline-first; on/off-ramp cost and country licensing apply.
