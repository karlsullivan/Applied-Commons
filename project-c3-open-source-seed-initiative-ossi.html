Open Source Seed Initiative (OSSI)
NeedFood and nutrition
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestropical, arid, temperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

A nonprofit 501(c)(3) building a 'protected commons' of open-source seed varieties through a public Pledge that grants four freedoms (save, grow, share, and improve, without patenting or licensing) and bars re-restriction of derivatives. Founded in 2012 by plant breeders, farmers, and seed companies, and active with dozens of breeders and seed-company partners. It addresses the production-inputs and nutritional-adequacy gaps by letting farmers and breeders freely develop and share locally-adapted crops - including cold-hardy varieties - for a more affordable local diet, free of seed-rights restrictions. Helps smallholders secure appropriate seed; low direct cost. (An open licensing/seed-commons project with a public Pledge, rather than a hardware repo.)
