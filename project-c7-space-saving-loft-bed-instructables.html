Space Saving Loft Bed (Instructables)
NeedSleep and rest
StatusDiscovered
LicenceNone found
This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open loft bed/desk combo (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, 8 steps) in 2x4/4x4 lumber plus bolts that raises the sleep surface and reclaims floor space in a small shared home (built by the author for a small house shared with a roommate). Helps sleepers in small, shared, or informal dwellings (space driver); passive, no power, hand-tool buildable (saw + drill). Mature: published 2015, featured, ~27K views, 450 favorites, with planning and build steps.
