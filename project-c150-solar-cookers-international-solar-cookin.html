This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open, community-edited wiki and toolkit (the John Collentine Solar Cooking Toolkit) with solar-cooker designs, build guides, materials, testing methods and training, organized by audience (beginner to national programme). Community-built library spanning 140+ countries in 35+ languages; free. Mature and documented clean-cooking/food know-how.
