Open-source ESPHome project (~66 stars, created 2025, active 2026) for a cheap (under EUR 25) full-featured alarm clock with Home Assistant integration: 4 HA switches triggered by alarm time, customizable sleep and snooze timers, radio, day/night display dimming. Locally buildable; helps smart-home users automate heating/lights around their sleep and alarm time.
