This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open Foris Initiative open-source tool for designing surveys and managing field-inventory data (forest, soil, carbon). Fast, multi-language, works offline/standalone in remote sites and as a Docker container, with controlled QA workflow and XML/CSV/DB export. Enables the restoration-monitoring requirement — publishing sapling-survival rates (only 5% of projects currently do) and biological-CDR MRV — for local teams in cold/remote boreal sites. MIT licence, active (last push 2026-11-10), part of a mature open-forest-inventory suite.
