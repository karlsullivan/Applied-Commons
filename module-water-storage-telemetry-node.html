This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Small off-grid node on the storage/elevated tank that tracks water level and (optionally) residual quality, warns of low level and long-stagnant water, and logs to the local bus; the monitoring layer that keeps the handpump source and the multi-barrier package's storage usable without internet or a phone.
