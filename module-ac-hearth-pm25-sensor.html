This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

The verification instrument the need brief explicitly requires: a simple, portable, low-cost PM2.5 (and PM10) node to confirm the achieved before/after reduction in the kitchen / breathing zone and to log continuous cooking-level PM2.5 against the WHO interim target 1 annual level of 35 ug/m3 (design trajectory toward the 2021 Global AQG 5 ug/m3). Built from an open, off-grid, USB/solar+battery-powered air-quality board (Sniffer / AirGradient-ONE / Project-Aura class: a laser PM2.5/PM10 part such as PMSA003 on an ESP32 with T/RH). Deliberately distinct from the existing fixed community ac-ambient-air-node: this one is hand-carried, portable, and tuned for transient high cooking-level exposures and before/after A/B comparison. Reports on a local bus so readings survive no-connectivity. The before/after protocol (fixed sensor placement, standardised cooking test, sensor-uncertainty cross-check, RFC 3339 UTC timestamps) is documented in this module's notes rather than proposed as a new standard, since the sensor-to-controller data exchange is already covered by the approved ac-mqtt-iot-bus + ac-iso8601-timestamps standards.
