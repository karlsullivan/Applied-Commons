This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

A shared, offline-first record that captures and verifies a technician's competency and certification per asset class (handpump, solar/energy, medical device), so a trained technician's qualifications travel with them across the training modules that produce them and across village and community-facility scenarios. Replaces the paper cards and spreadsheets the source projects currently use and makes the 'trained technician' actually verifiable.
