This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open repair-guide knowledge base: 72,178 board- and part-level guides across 46,800 devices from 69 manufacturers, imported from iFixit/Repair Wiki or written there, with declared licences, member guides reviewed before publication; includes a medical-equipment section (6,192 devices, 360 guides); mature at scale, serves the electronics right-to-repair and LMIC legacy-equipment repair gap.
