Hikma Health

Last updated upstreamNovember 2026
This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Free, open-source, offline-first electronic health record for clinics serving refugees, migrants and low-income patients; records are stored on-device and sync opportunistically when connectivity returns, in Arabic/English/Spanish, with pharmacy and role-based privacy. The site reports 1.3M patients served across 23 countries by 2025. Directly meets the brief's offline-first patient-continuity requirement. Open source (mobile/backend components are MIT on GitHub), actively maintained (repos pushed 2025-2026).
