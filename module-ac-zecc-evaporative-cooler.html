This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Non-electric evaporative (zeer) cooler for the zero-electricity, dry-climate perishable tier: two local clay pots, wet sand and a wet cloth, built from local materials for about US$1 with no grid, refrigerant or fuel, maintained by watering 1-3x/day. Extends perishable shelf life 2.5-4x over ambient (ripe tomato 6 to 15 days; >8 C real-world drop in the MIT CITE/D-Lab Mali sensor study). Documented at scale in 100,000+ pots in northern Nigeria and trials in Mali, Burkina Faso, Gambia and Ethiopia. Serves R4 and the cheapest end of R1.
