HDPE Bag Plug-Flow Biogas Digester

HDPE Bag Plug-Flow Biogas Digester
Kindhardware
Maturitydocumented
NeedsSanitation and hygiene
This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

A self-contained anaerobic plug-flow digester where an HDPE pondliner 'bag' forms the gas-tight vessel over an in-ground/brick-lined pit, sized parametrically from an open spreadsheet (feed type, retention time, C/N). Produces cooking biogas + liquid digestate/fertiliser from livestock and human waste, with zero discharge to the surface. Serves small-farm, homestead and village scenarios. The parametric sizing sheet and drawings are the reusable, shareable core.
