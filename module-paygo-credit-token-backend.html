This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Server-side credit engine that holds customer balances, generates and validates the ac-paygo-credit-token top-ups, and manages meter state (consumption, credit-remaining, arrears) for revenue and receivables. Offline-first so a network outage does not stop top-ups; the collection/management layer for the Ghana ~US$33 arrears problem (lift recovery from 66 cents to >=95 cents). Meter-agnostic - drives the prepaid meter terminal.
