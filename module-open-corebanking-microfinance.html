This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Cloud-ready core-banking system for microfinance institutions serving the unbanked: to-harvest short-term credit for smallholders and agri-SMEs, group lending, and mobile-money repayment rails. Provides the loan/credit ledger the health-shock liquidity, PAYGo installment financing and input-credit needs settle against, at a total cost below the value of the inputs it funds.
