Open-source, carbon-negative low-speed electric-vehicle (LSEV) platform from wood, developed in Latvia (Rihards Gailums / WoodpeckerCar), with open frame drawings and open electronics firmware (MIT/Apache-licensed repos for CAN controller, LED/light-sound controller, throttle, ultrasonic and brake modules). It is a flat-pack, modular 'skateboard' platform (25 mph / 40 km/h) intended to carry cargo or passengers in manual, tele-operated or autonomous mode, targeting urban micromobility and agriculture/industrial logistics. COLD-CLIMATE CANDIDATE: developed in Latvia, a long-freezing-winter, low-winter-sun region (basis is design/origin geography; the site states it is intended for 'controlled environment' use, so it is a platform/prototype, not a field-proven all-terrain vehicle).
