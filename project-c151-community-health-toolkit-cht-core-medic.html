This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source (CHT Core, AGPL-3.0) framework that 'makes it faster to build responsive, offline-first digital health apps that equip health workers to provide better care in their communities,' built on top of an offline-first data model. Aimed explicitly at the hardest-to-reach areas, with open design, technical and implementer resources so local teams can build and deploy. Maturity: high (medic/cht-core, 557 stars, active; supported by a consortium of global-health organizations).
