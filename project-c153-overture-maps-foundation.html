This project is under research: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Linux Foundation open-data mapping collaboration (launched Dec 2022, founding members Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, TomTom) that fuses OSM with 200+ other open sources into documented open datasets — addresses, base (infrastructure, land cover/use, water features), 2B+ building footprints, administrative divisions, places, transportation — under CDLA-Permissive 2.0 where possible and ODbL v1.0 for OSM-derived themes, with open-source (typically MIT) tooling on GitHub. A standing, industry-funded (not donor-funded) open basemap layer complementary to OSM that governments and NGOs can download and own locally; active as of schema-repo commit 2026-11-20.
