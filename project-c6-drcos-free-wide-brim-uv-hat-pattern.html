DRCOS Free Wide-Brim UV Hat Pattern

From Applied Commons, the open catalogue of ways to meet human needs
DRCOS Free Wide-Brim UV Hat Pattern
NeedClothing and bodily protection
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestropical, arid, temperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Free, downloadable actual-size sewing pattern plus tutorial for a wide-brim hat explicitly intended for UV protection, in graded head sizes from 44 cm to 64 cm (child to adult), with seam allowances included. Helps anyone able to hand- or machine-sew who needs broad-brimmed UV head/face/neck coverage; uses common medium-weight fabrics. Maturity: free and published, but a single independent pattern with no measured UPF test reported.
