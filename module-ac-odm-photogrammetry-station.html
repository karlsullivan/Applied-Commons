This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Command-line open photogrammetry engine (AGPL-3.0, community-run since 2014) that turns drone/balloon/kite photos into georeferenced orthophotos (to 5 cm), DEMs/DSMs, 3D textured models and point clouds. Runs headless on a self-hosted workstation; documented accuracy of 0.7-1.2 m horizontal without GCPs and 1.5-35 cm RMSE with 5 GCPs across consumer/RTK tiers, 15-35 min runtimes on i7/16 GB-class hardware. Field-validated in a cold alpine glacier (Kanderfirn, ~2300-3600 m, 2017/18) and a tropical humanitarian deployment (HOT Freetown, Sierra Leone: 80 km2 at 5 cm from 130,000+ images, local crew trained, outputs published openly). Closes the donor-dependent UNOSAT imagery/analysis gap with a community re-runnable local stack.
