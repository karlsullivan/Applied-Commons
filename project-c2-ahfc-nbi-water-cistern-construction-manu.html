Open public design manual (translated from the Norwegian Building Research Institute 'Byggdetaljer' series, produced with the University of Alaska Fairbanks) covering construction, siting, and maintenance of a cistern for collecting and storing rainwater for household use in sub-freezing coastal climates. It documents the year-round cold-climate details that make storage work through long freezing winters: frost protection of the cistern and filter with heat tape and a temperature sensor placed inside the cistern, insulation of the filter together with the tank, and an option to bypass the filter in winter so roof water still reaches the tank. Gravity/manual, no grid power, locally buildable - it is the storage/delivery half of the path and is explicitly for the low-sun, long-winter settings the brief targets.
