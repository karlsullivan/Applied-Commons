This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open Python models (with optional R/Shiny GUIs) to predict how well granular-activated-carbon (GAC) and ion-exchange (IX) units remove dissolved chemical contaminants - arsenic, lead, fluoride, PFAS, TCE, algal organic matter - from water; used to decide whether and how to add a chemical-removal stage to the multi-barrier drinking-water package where fluoride/arsenic/lead are present (per the need-brief's WHO chemical targets).
