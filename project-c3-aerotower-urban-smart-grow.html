This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source, low-cost and hackable vertical aeroponics tower (model, electronics and firmware intended to be fully open). 3D-printable on a machine as small as a Prusa Mini, parts screw together, and it is designed to be self-sufficient on a solar panel and connect as an IoT device. Positioned as a cheap entry point into aeroponics that can be scaled up to a full farm, indoor or outdoor, independent of weather. Maturity: active community project with published build documentation; early-stage (lighting is deliberately an add-on rather than included).
