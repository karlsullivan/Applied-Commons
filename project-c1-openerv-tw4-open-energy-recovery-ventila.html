This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source through-wall decentralized energy recovery ventilator designed in Canada (Ottawa) for year-round cold-climate use: transfers fresh outdoor air in and polluted air out while recovering ~85% sensible and ~80% latent heat, ~60 CFM net fresh air, 37 dBa on medium. Two modules (WM12) fit in a window; open CAD, Python firmware and build docs. Developer notes the project is on hold (June 2025) while he focuses on mass manufacturing another product, so it is a mature design but not actively maintained. Cold-climate: yes, designed explicitly for Canadian winters.
