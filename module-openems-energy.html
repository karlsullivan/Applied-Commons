AGPL-3.0 open-source energy-management system (OpenEMS Edge) that monitors PV, battery and loads over Modbus/MQTT and dispatches storage to keep priority loads running; sits on a community-owned host and consumes ac-pv-array-input-rev-202610 for the PV side.
