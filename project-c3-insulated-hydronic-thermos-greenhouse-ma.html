Open-source (CERN-OHL-S v2) zero-grid, R-30-insulated year-round food-production greenhouse for sub-zero climates. Replaces glazing with a highly insulated SIP / 2x6 envelope and ceiling tubular daylighting devices (99.7% reflective) that pipe in low-angle winter sun; an active hydronic 300-gallon water thermal battery under elevated crop benches keeps root zones warm through nights down to -15F / -26C. The whole climate loop is a pure analog bimetallic relay - zero microcontrollers, zero software, ultra-low electrical power - so it runs without a reliable grid or internet. Maturity: early - V1.0 design document (2026, ~9 commits), but the most complete open severe-cold design found.
