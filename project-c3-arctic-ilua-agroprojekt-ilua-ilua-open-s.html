Open-source passive-thermal greenhouse system for Arctic / extreme-cold food production (agroprojekt-ilua / ilua.gl). Modular construction with passive solar and thermal-energy-storage (TES), prototyped in South Greenland, with all work published. Directly targets year-round growing through long freezing, low-sun winters - one of only a handful of genuinely open cold-climate growing designs. Maturity: early - Field Season 1 (2026) in site establishment and engineering study; license stated as MIT/CC-BY-SA (TBD).
