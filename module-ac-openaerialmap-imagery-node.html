This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open platform (HOT, since 2015; 5,000+ users, 100+ images/month) that hosts, shares and serves openly-licensed satellite and drone imagery for humanitarian mapping, used in Nepal 2015, Turkey-Syria 2023 and Myanmar. Being rebuilt with Development Seed on a STAC-based open stack (COG, pgSTAC, TiTiler) so a local instance can index imagery from open providers and serve standard TMS tiles - closing the imagery-access gap otherwise held by UNOSAT-EMS. Distributes the ODM station's orthophoto output and supplies basemaps to the field-collection and editing-station modules.
