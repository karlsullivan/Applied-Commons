This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open platform node (HOT OpenAerialMap, since 2015; 5,000+ users, 100+ images/month) that hosts, shares and serves openly-licensed satellite and drone imagery for humanitarian mapping, as used in Nepal 2015, Turkey-Syria 2023 and Myanmar. Being rebuilt on a modern open stack; tiles and metadata stay ODbL/CC-licensed and locally served.
