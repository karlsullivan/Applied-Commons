This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Vendor- and technology-agnostic open-source home/automation bus (pure Java, OSGi) that runs as the central controller for a wide range of devices and protocols, with bindings including Modbus and Matter. Mature, documented, and deployable on commodity hardware (including Raspberry Pi), it offers an open control layer that can drive pumps, valves and other actuators without lock-in to a single vendor cloud. Works year-round in cold-climate homes as a locally hosted controller.
