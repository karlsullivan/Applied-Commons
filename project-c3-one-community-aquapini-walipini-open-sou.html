This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Free-shared (project-launch blueprinting) in-ground 'aquapini' designs: 6 in-ground structures (~10,622 sq ft planted) sunk below the frost line for a stable ~55F/13C year-round temperature, with a large-scale food-production aquapini plus backyard and maximally-affordable walipini versions; free AutoCAD/SketchUp downloads offered. Maturity: explicitly blueprint/incomplete phase (site invites help finishing the designs), not yet built. Note: their demonstration site is in California (USDA zone 7a/7b); the cold-resilience comes from the below-frost-line thermal design, not the site.
