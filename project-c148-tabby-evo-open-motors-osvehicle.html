This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source modular electric-vehicle hardware platform by Open Motors (formerly OSVehicle), released under Creative Commons BY-SA 4.0 and free for personal, academic and commercial use, with downloadable 2D/3D CAD files and a public specification + Bill of Materials brochure. It is a complete drivetrain kit (chassis, suspension, steering, brakes, 19 kW AC motor, BMS, 12.8 kWh LFP battery) that assembles in about an hour and is used to build 2-4 seat low-speed and mid-speed electric vehicles. Maturity: mature and sold (project since 2013, EVO generation 2015+), though Euro/low-speed oriented rather than rugged rural-track oriented, and not a cold-climate candidate.
