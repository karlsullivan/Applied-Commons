This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

A local data-sovereignty layer: consent registry, role-based access control, FHIR de-identification (removal of the 18 HIPAA direct identifiers + k-anonymity on quasi-identifiers), and a tamper-evident audit log. Gives governments and communities local ownership of health and personal data - the brief's explicit requirement - instead of cloud-first routing through external clouds.
