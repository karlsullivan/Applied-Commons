OSE Open-Source Portable Forced-Induction Thermoelectric Solid-Fuel Stove

From Applied Commons, the open catalogue of ways to meet human needs
OSE Open-Source Portable Forced-Induction Thermoelectric Solid-Fuel Stove
NeedAir and breathing
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestropical, arid, temperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source design (BOM, CAD, code) for a portable fan-forced gasifier stove with an integrated thermoelectric generator that self-powers the fan - explicitly positioned as a maintainable, modular open alternative to BioLite-style commercial stoves. Forced induction gives a better fuel-air ratio, less smoke and less CO. Prototype-stage design documentation; locally buildable in principle, needs a fan and battery/TEG module.
