USGS coupled Coastal Ocean and Atmospheric Wave-Structure Terrain (COAWST) modeling system, in the US public domain (fully open). Simulates storm surge, waves and nearshore circulation for coastal hazard and adaptation analysis. 163 stars, active (pushed 2026-10-29).
