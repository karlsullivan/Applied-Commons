This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Self-hosted, offline-capable web app for caregivers to log and review family and infant sleep, feeds and routines, with local data ownership (CSV/XLSX in and out) and no subscription. Targets the caregiver/infant sleep need specifically (and, by the same design, shared household sleep routines), where commercial apps hold the data behind accounts; runs on a modest VPS or a local box and has a Home Assistant integration so it can feed the hub.
