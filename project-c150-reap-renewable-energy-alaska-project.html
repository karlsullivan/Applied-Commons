REAP (Renewable Energy Alaska Project)
NeedSkills and know-how
ServesContinuity of essential services, Economic and livelihood security, Environmental stability and resource security
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatescold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Nonprofit delivering clean-energy workforce training and education (wind, solar, small hydro, energy storage) for Alaska communities, schools and businesses, preparing people for applied-technical energy jobs. Works year-round in a long-freezing, low-winter-sun climate (Alaska). Mature, documented, local.
