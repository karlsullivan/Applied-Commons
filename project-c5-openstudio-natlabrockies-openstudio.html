Cross-platform building-modeling toolkit built on EnergyPlus plus Radiance daylight analysis (649 stars, pushed 2026-10-01; developed by NREL) for envelope, loads and HVAC design — the open design toolkit for getting a building's thermal and daylighting behavior right before construction.
