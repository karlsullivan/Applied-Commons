Unless otherwise stated, hardware designs in this repository are licensed under the **CERN Open Hardware Licence Version 2 – Strongly Reciprocal** (SPDX: `CERN-OHL-S-2.0`).

Hardware designs include design files for physical things: mechanical drawings and CAD models, schematics and PCB layouts, bills of materials, and the module and system specifications they are built from.

Anyone may use, study, modify, make, share and sell the designs and products made from them, including commercially. If you distribute a modified design, or products made from one, you must make the modified design files available under the same licence.

https://ohwr.org/cern_ohl_s_v2.txt
