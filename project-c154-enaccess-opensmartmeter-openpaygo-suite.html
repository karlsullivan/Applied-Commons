This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source (MIT / Apache-2.0) hardware and software from EnAccess, a solar-off-grid industry body: an inexpensive, OEM-agnostic GSM prepaid smart meter plus the OpenPAYGO Suite (hardware- and software-agnostic PAYGo token technology, active since 2022). Public hardware and firmware designs are locally buildable and repairable, far below the ~US$1,000 commercial prepaid terminal. Fits energy PAYGo micro-installments and open prepaid water metering (offline balance top-ups). Caveat: GSM/LoRa radio and token backend needed; not fully offline end-to-end, and a missed payment can cut supply by default.
