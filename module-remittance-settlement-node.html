This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Application/anchor node on the Stellar network providing a fast, low-cost cross-border remittance and wage-transfer rail with end-to-end cost well under the ~6% global average (target <2%) and no unexpected fees on receipt. Serves the coping/safety remittance channel and the settlement layer for the core-banking and non-custodial savings modules.
