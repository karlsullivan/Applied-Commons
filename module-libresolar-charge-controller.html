Open-hardware MPPT solar charge controller (Libre Solar 'Flexible' architecture) for off-grid battery banks: configurable current limit per array size, locally serviceable with common tools, with build documentation from the project's open educational resource. The charging front-end of household DC systems, cold rooms, health-facility banks and pump charge paths.
