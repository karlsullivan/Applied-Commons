Open, locally-run data platform (EmonCMS, AGPL-3.0) that ingests (Mosquitto MQTT, REST, RF), durably stores and serves (Graph/Dashboard/REST/CSV) continuous environmental + energy telemetry so readings survive disconnection and stay comparable across deployments.
