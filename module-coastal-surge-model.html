This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open, field-calibrated prediction layer for site-specific coastal defence design: a 2D numerical model of short- and long-wave (infragravity) transformation, wave-induced setup, unsteady currents, overwash and inundation, and morphodynamics (bed and suspended sediment transport, dune-face avalanching, bed update, breaching), with explicit effects of vegetation and hard structures. Used to compare protection-per-metre and cost of nature-based (mangrove/marsh) versus grey defences under storm surge and sea-level rise, supporting the brief's coastal-defence cost-vs-protection requirement.
