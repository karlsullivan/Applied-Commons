This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Local open energy management system (OpenEMS Edge on a community-owned host): monitors PV, battery and loads over Modbus/MQTT and dispatches storage to keep priority loads running 24/7 - first the cold chain, then lighting and communications - at health facilities and small microgrids; time-of-use and peak-shaving logic cuts lifetime delivered cost versus diesel generation.
