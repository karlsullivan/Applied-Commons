This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source (MIT, 25 stars) process-based ecosystem model of vegetation, organic soil and permafrost carbon from the University of Alaska Fairbanks; repo demo data is from the Toolik Field Station, Alaska. Strongest cold-climate candidate: directly models the carbon pools and thaw risk in long-freezing, low-sun arctic/boreal landscapes.
