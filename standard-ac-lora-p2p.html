Meshtastic LoRa mesh for off-grid text messages

StatusApproved
ApprovedOctober 2026

A Meshtastic mesh of cheap LoRa radios passes text and positions between phones and nodes for kilometres with no towers, internet or subscription, and is field-proven at community scale. The text keeps its limits honest: short messages, keys that are easy to share, and no way to stop a key holder sending, which is why warnings over it are advisory.

Specification

Off-grid text and position messages between people and nodes use the Meshtastic protocol and firmware (GPL-3.0) on LoRa radios, with no base station or internet. A text message is at most 200 bytes, so warnings are kept short. Channels: the default primary channel uses a publicly known key and is treated as public. Each deployment adds a community channel with its own 256-bit pre-shared key, shared only with members. Meshtastic cannot separate the right to read a channel from the right to send on it, and channel messages are not signed: anyone holding a channel's key can send on it under any name. So a warning received over the mesh is advisory: it carries the CAP alert identifier (ac-early-warning-message) so it can be checked against other channels, and the mesh is never the only path for a life-safety warning. Radio: the region preset matching the national rules (in Europe the 869.4-869.65 MHz sub-band, up to 500 mW ERP and 10 % duty cycle under ETSI EN 300 220), with the firmware's duty-cycle limits left on. Gateways bridge chosen channels to the local MQTT broker (ac-mqtt-iot-bus).
