This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open (no-patent) terracotta point-of-use filter: local clay + sawdust/rice husk, pressed and fired locally (~860 degC), in a lidded bucket with tap (1.5-2.5 L/hr); colloidal silver for ~100% of bacterial indicators. The locally-sourced, no-grid, no-municipal-infrastructure water option - the complement to Faircap where local clay firing is available. Strongest evidence base of the recovered set (RCT + CDC + field studies).
