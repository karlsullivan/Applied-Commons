This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source warehouse and inventory management system designed for NGO and humanitarian supply chains: stock, donations, assets and distribution tracking with a documented low-cost implementation track record. 932 stars, pushed 2026-11-18, EPL-1.0 licensed. Fits offline-first consignment tracking and last-mile stock record-keeping.
