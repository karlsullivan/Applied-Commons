This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Free, hand-knit wool-insulated mukluk (soft-soled slipper-boot) pattern derived from traditional Arctic footwear, with gauge specs, yarn recommendations, foot measurements for sizes XS-XL and hand-sewn finishing. Helps cold-climate makers with a fully hand-knitted, needle-and-wool-only insulated footwear pattern. Maturity: free, tested and published; designed for dry/indoor soft-surface wear (14-18 h per pair) rather than a wet-outdoor puncture-proof sole.
