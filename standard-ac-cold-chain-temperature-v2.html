Cold chain temperatures, monitoring and prequalification scope

Cold chain temperatures, monitoring and prequalification scope
Codeac-cold-chain-temperature-v2
Kindthermal
StatusApproved
ApprovedNovember 2026

Citing the correct PQS category (E001 for cold rooms, E003 for freezers) is a no-cost fix that removes a real assurance gap: a cold room cannot be validated against E003, and today's single-category mandate would let an unvalidated cold room pass review. Scoping the standard to genuine cold-chain modules costs nothing to the seven real modules and stops the sleep/ambient/comfort modules from being forced to satisfy vaccine-grade logging, alarms and PQS prequalification they do not need - which is what is currently forcing workarounds and inflating the adoption count. Cost and availability are unchanged: the same EN 12830/13485/13486 loggers and the same WHO PQS prequalification paths are used, so no genuine module changes its hardware; only the cold-room prequalification clause and the tags of the five non-cold-chain modules change.

Specification

Vaccine storage: +2 to +8 C in equipment prequalified by WHO PQS for the correct category - E003 'Refrigerators and freezers' for compact units (including solar direct-drive) and E001 'Cold rooms, freezer rooms, and related equipment' for cold rooms; each with a temperature monitoring device meeting WHO PQS E006 (e.g. a 30-day electronic logger, WHOPQS-approved sensor type). An Applied Commons vaccine-fridge or vaccine-cold-room design does not replace a prequalified one in a health programme until evaluated against the matching E003 or E001 category. Food: chilled at or below the national limit (commonly +5 C or +8 C); frozen at -18 C or colder. Temperature recorders/thermometers for food storage and transport meet EN 12830 and EN 13485, verified per EN 13486. Every genuine cold-chain module logs temperature at least every 15 minutes, alarms on any excursion, publishes readings on ac-mqtt-iot-bus, and keeps a local record that survives a power cut. Designs state holdover time at their hottest rated ambient and freeze protection (sub-zero ambients included in the cold-climate set). This standard applies only to modules whose primary purpose is storing vaccines, food, or perishables in a controlled-temperature chain; modules that only incidentally report an ambient, room, or body temperature (home-climate, weather-station, sleep/actigraphy, white-noise) are out of scope and are not counted toward adoption of this standard.
