This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source, single-lead ECG in a business-card form factor, with open hardware (two KiCad 7 PCB designs: a carrier board and a solder-on ECG frontend with ADC and touch detection) and open software. A low-cost, locally buildable point-of-care cardiac sensor that makes dominant cardiovascular conditions detectable without a hospital. Covers the health sensing requirement.
