This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

A validated mesophilic (works best ~37 C) masonry plug-flow digester, 300x100x100 cm exterior / 270x70x100 cm interior (~1.8 m3, liquid height ~90 cm), covered with a pond-foil gas bag attached 40 cm below the rim. Processes human and animal waste into cooking biogas + high-quality organic fertiliser for small farms/homesteads in warm climates. Shares the same feed/gas/digestate interface standards as the bag-biodigester so the two can swap.
