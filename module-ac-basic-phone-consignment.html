This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Tracks one consignment from district warehouse to last use with <=3 status updates per day on a BASIC mobile phone (2G SMS/USSD + LoRa-P2P fallback), no continuous connectivity, usable by a 15-year-old after 1 hour of training. Interoperates with the existing ac-fleetbase-dispatch over its REST + Webhook API; the district side runs on a self-hosted Fleetbase node.
