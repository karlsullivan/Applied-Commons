New Frameworks Natural Building - Straw Bale Buildings Research Program (Vermont)
NeedShelter and thermal comfort
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatesarid, temperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Documented, measured cold-climate study of straw-bale wall energy performance (final report, December 2011; New Frameworks Natural Building LLC, Natural Design/Build, and Building Performance Services, all in Vermont, USA; testing performed with Building Performance Services and Advanced Energy Solutions). Provides performance evidence that straw-bale envelopes hold a comfortable indoor band in a long-freezing-winter climate without grid-based heating.
