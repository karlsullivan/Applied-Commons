Assemble a modular rotary drive
IDPROC19
IndustryMetals, machine tools and common components IND05
StatusConceptual process route
EvidenceInferred
Approved to buildNo
A conceptual route: its quantities, yields and capacity are unknown, so it is not a build or operating instruction, and no capacity, mass balance or cost can be calculated from it yet.

Drive energy source is selected through component alternatives; a universal interface is proposed, not proven.
