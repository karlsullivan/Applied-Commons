This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open (MIT) fully-documented plan for a single-garage, off-grid plastic processing/recovery lab: 7 systems (ultrasonic cleaner, shredder/sorter, filament extruder, microwave pyrolysis, PET chemical recovery, plasma carbon converter, mold press) plus a 4-stage wash-water treatment train, with working Python planning/simulation models and Arduino control code. A deeper (chemical + water-treatment) complement to the mechanical pp-plastic-recycling-line for sites that can run it; NOT yet built - the plan/models are the deliverable.
