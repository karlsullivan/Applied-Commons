This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Cargo-trike-mounted 100-200 L water tank or a 10-person WASH kit (hand-washing station + portable latrine + hygiene kit) delivered to the point of use within 10 km. Food-grade tank, DN-threaded fill/drain, gravity-drain outlet, and a water-treatment-performance checkpoint at the point of use. Cost target <USD 0.50 per 100 L per trip over the asset's useful life.
