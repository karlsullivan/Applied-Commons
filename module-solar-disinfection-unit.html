This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

No-power solar disinfection of pre-filtered water using PET bottles (or an open tray) on a rack: UV-A plus heat inactivate bacteria and reduce protozoan load over the required dwell time; the dedicated disinfection barrier of the multi-barrier package, addressing the virus/protozoa gap.
