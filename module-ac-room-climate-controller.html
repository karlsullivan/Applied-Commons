This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Local, cloud-free room-temperature controller that drives a heating, cooling and fan load to hold a setpoint, built on ESPHome's climate platforms (bang_bang, dual-point thermostat, and PID with Ziegler-Nichols autotune). No subscription, no cloud dependency; the ESP32 publishes climate state to the local MQTT bus and accepts setpoint commands from the home climate hub.
