This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Local, cloud-free room-temperature controller that drives a heating, cooling and fan load to hold a setpoint, built on ESPHome's climate platforms (bang_bang, dual-point thermostat, and PID with Ziegler-Nichols autotune). No subscription, sub-euro-30 parts, USB-reflashable and repairable from off-the-shelf parts. Mains-safe operation is builder-implemented, because ESPHome ships no interlock, over-temperature trip, over-current protection or fail-safe: an SPDT hardware interlock, an over-temp trip, a manual override and an active-low fail-safe relay are part of the build.
