Climatestropical, arid, temperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

German (Berlin) open-hardware environmental monitoring station (solar/battery powered, field-rugged) that plugs in modular air, water and soil sensors, and pushes readings to openSenseMap, an open-data platform that exposes billions of measurements under Public Domain Dedication 1.0 with an open API. Operates year-round in cold climates, supports local data storage and low backhaul, and is a strong cold-climate candidate for the open-data-and-calibration requirement.
