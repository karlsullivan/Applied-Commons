This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Locally-buildable gentle wake alarm that replaces the abrupt phone alarm (linked to a higher morning blood-pressure surge) with a crescendo audio or light-fade wake, timed to the user's sleep episode so it fires within a wake window. Open hardware + firmware, no subscription; the wake time is taken from the standard sleep episode so the alarm tracks the actual last sleep period rather than a fixed clock time.
