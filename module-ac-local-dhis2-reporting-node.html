This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

A documented, single-box deployment pack for DHIS2 (the national open health-information platform in 80+ LMICs) that the ministry of health runs in-country: district+facility hierarchy, offline-first data entry, and program reporting with no dependency on external cloud. Closes the brief's gap that DHIS2 stops at the district level and the lowest level stays 'complicated and costly' because LMICs lack IT planners.
