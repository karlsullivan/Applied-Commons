HALO (open-source ESP32 air quality monitor)
NeedAir and breathing
StatusScreened
LicenceOther
Sourcegithub.com
Last updated upstreamSeptember 2026
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

ESP32-S3 monitor measuring PM1/2.5/4/10, VOC, CO2 and multiple gases (NO2, NH3, CH4, CO, H2, ethanol) with a 3.4in touchscreen, 11-LED RGB ring, ESPHome and Home Assistant support, plus a browser-based firmware installer. Open-source firmware and CAD files; actively maintained (~948 stars, last push 2026-09).
