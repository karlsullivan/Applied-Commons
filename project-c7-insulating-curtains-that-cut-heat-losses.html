This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open sewn roll-up insulating curtains (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, 14 steps, authored by a mechanical/environmental engineer) that the author states cut window heat loss by ~50%; the opaque fabric also blocks interior light toward blackout, covering the light driver. Helps cold-climate households and anyone needing a dark, warm bedroom; passive, hand/sewing-tool buildable, hardware- and fabric-store materials. Mature: published 2014, featured winner, ~331K views. Note: designed for heat loss (cold climate); in hot climates open them or use the reflective side so they do not trap heat.
