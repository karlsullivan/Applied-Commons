This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Self-hosted aggregation layer that ingests field signals (Ushahidi reports, sensor nodes, community SMS/LoRa reports), correlates them, and emits CAP 1.2 early-warning alerts to community channels (SMS, WhatsApp, radio, mesh). Bridges ac-ushahidi-crisis-mapping-node and the sensor/dispatch ecosystem.
