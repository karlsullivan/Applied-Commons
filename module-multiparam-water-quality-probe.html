This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Self-contained probe for turbidity, temperature, TDS/salinity, pH, dissolved oxygen and (optionally) free residual chlorine across source, treatment and storage points; shares sensors and the calibration path with the treatment package, storage node and handpump sensor.
