This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Genuinely new module filling the one unmet cold/polar gap in the domain: a process-based model of vegetation, dynamic organic soil and permafrost carbon (the boreal/arctic belt holds half the world's soil carbon). Produces per-cell estimates of carbon pools and thaw risk in long-freezing, low-sun landscapes - the slice none of the five existing modules covers. Wraps UAF DVM (Dynamic Vegetation, Dynamic Organic Soil, Terrestrial Ecosystem Model); repo demo data is from Toolik Field Station, Alaska.
