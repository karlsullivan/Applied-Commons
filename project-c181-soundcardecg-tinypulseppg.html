This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Low-cost point-of-care health instrumentation: SoundCardECG (MIT) is an open design that records ECG through a sound-card/AD8232 interface with a documented build, and tinyPulsePPG (Unlicense) is an open ATTiny85 pulse-oximeter/PPG with display for SpO2/heart rate. Both are cheap, open and locally buildable, matching the low-cost ECG + oximetry-at-point-of-care requirement; they are non-clinical/hobby-grade and would need validation before any life-safety use.

References

  1. Comparative Assessment of AD8232- and MAX30003-Based ECG Acquisition Paths Using Synthetic ECG Signals (Acta of Bioengineering and Biomechanics, 2026) - Pearson correlation >0.99 at 30-100 bpm synthetic signals, lab-level europepmc.org
  2. Optimized Performance Pulse Oximeter Based on the MAX30102 Commercial Sensor (IFMBE Proceedings, 2024) - prototype accuracy +/-1.39% SpO2, +/-2.04 bpm, Bland-Altman comparable to high-end reference oximeter (pilot test) link.springer.com
  3. Design validation of single lead AD8232 analog ECG module with a PC based sound card for analysis in comparison to standard ECG monitor (IJST, 2021) - n=80, r=0.9 p<=0.001 vs certified Maestros Magic-R MRX-12, ~Rs 1200 build manuscriptcommunicator.com
  4. Peri-operative pulse oximetry in low-income countries: a cost-effectiveness analysis (PMC4264392) - hand-held oximeter USD 115/DALY averted vs tabletop USD 374; 51-70% of low-income-country operating theatres lack oximetry; 77,000 theatres globally without oximetry pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
