Kindhardware
Maturitydocumented
NeedsClothing and bodily protection
This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Self-contained 3D-printed splash/droplet barrier for face and eyes: a printed closed headband holding a replaceable Mylar/PET face sheet, with a bleach-soak decontamination protocol. Extends scarce N95 viability and protects frontline and healthcare workers where imported PPE is scarce. Repairable (reprint) and buildable with commodity FDM printers and no connectivity.
