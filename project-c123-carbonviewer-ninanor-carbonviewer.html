MIT-licensed R Shiny application (Natural Resources Canada) that calculates peatland volume and carbon stock from GIS data to support area planners and decision makers - open MRV / durable carbon storage for peatlands, a cold-climate ecosystem (Canada, Scotland, Alaska). 8 stars (pushed 2025-12-05).
