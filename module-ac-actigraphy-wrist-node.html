This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Cheap, locally-buildable wrist node that records 3-axis actigraphy (5-min epoch counts) over 7+ days with a wrist-skin-temperature channel and on-device provisional sleep/wake detection; the capture half of the documented actigraphy pipeline that feeds the actigraphy engine. No subscription, no cloud, user-owned record. Wrist skin (distal) temperature is a validated sleep-onset and circadian marker, so the node logs it at least every 15 minutes with an excursion alarm and a local record that survives a power cut (thermal-monitoring standard).
