Root cellar - open, documented underground food-storage technique

Root cellar - open, documented underground food-storage technique
NeedFood and nutrition
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestemperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

A passive, no-power, underground or partially-underground food-storage structure (also called fruit cellar or earth cellar), fully documented as a public design (construction, ventilation, materials) with no intellectual-property restrictions and no app dependence. Holds most crops at 0-2 C and 90-95% relative humidity, keeping food from freezing in winter and cool in summer - a year-round cold-climate preservation solution that cuts post-harvest loss on roots, squash, onions, cabbage, etc. Helps smallholders and rural households; low material cost, buildable with masonry/digging skills, and operable without reliable grid power. (Distinct from the already-known 'OpenRoots' project; this is the documented building technique.)
