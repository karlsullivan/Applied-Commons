This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) web tool to aid the management and maintenance of medical devices (Vue / Node / Docker, with branching strategy and developer docs). Maturity: early-stage — v0.4.0, started 2021, ~1 star and 24 open issues; strong fit for the health requirement (device maintenance know-how for LMIC facilities) but weak on maturity and adoption, so treat as a seed rather than a scaled programme.
