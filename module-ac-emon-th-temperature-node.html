This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open-source Raspberry Pi/Arduino temperature-humidity and energy-monitoring stack (EmonLib, emonTH/emonTH2 and emonPi/emonPi3 hardware, EmonCMS web app) that closes the cheap, high-value monitoring and off-grid PV energy-accounting layer of the cold chain: continuous, recorded, calibratable and alarmable temperature plus energy accounting, replacing manual chart monitoring and uncalibrated, unalarmed sensors. Does not itself cool, back up power or store. Serves the monitoring facet of R1/R2/R3 and energy accounting for R1.
