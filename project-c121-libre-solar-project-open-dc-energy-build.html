This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source hardware and software building blocks for DC off-grid energy systems - flexible solar charge controllers, a 16S/100A battery management system, and an energy-management layer - plus a full open educational resource (learn.libre.solar) on building and producing the components. A European/German open-hardware project (a German Prototype Fund project and presented at the 2024 Chaos Communication Congress in Berlin), developed in a long-winter, low-winter-sun climate. Helps off-grid households and local builders get serviceable solar + storage for lighting, phone charging and a base for small loads; the open design and OER make it locally maintainable with common tools and it supports the brief's storage-management and lighting/charging needs.
