This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open community training manual for handpump maintenance (Inter Aide Sierra Leone, in the Rural Water Supply Network library): discussion questions, repair and preventive-maintenance guidelines, water-committee duties and practical exercises. A locally usable, low-cost skills package for training and retaining village handpump technicians against the sub-Saharan non-functionality gap.
