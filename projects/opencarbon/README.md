# OpenCarbon

**Status: first active technical stream; initial scoping.**

OpenCarbon explores practical greenhouse-gas removal, durable carbon storage, and measurement, reporting, and verification. The intended public benefit is reproducible evidence about approaches that can deliver real environmental outcomes.

## Initial scope

- Review existing research and technologies, and select a bounded question to test.
- Develop designs and experiments that others can independently build and evaluate.
- Measure removal or storage alongside relevant inputs, energy use, emissions, uncertainty, and durability limits. State the system boundary; do not equate gross capture with net removal.

No specific technology, prototype performance, or verified removal outcome is established by this project record.

## Expected outputs and success

Publish a project brief, designs and bills of materials, test methods, raw data, analysis, and documented failures where possible. Define numerical success criteria before testing. Useful progress means reproducible measurements that support or reject an approach, including its limitations.

Work may require process or chemical engineering, instrumentation, environmental measurement, and data analysis expertise, depending on the chosen experiment. Assess hazards before selecting equipment or methods.

Use the [project](../../templates/PROJECT.md) and [experiment](../../templates/EXPERIMENT.md) templates. Follow [contribution guidance](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) and [safety guidance](../../SAFETY.md).
