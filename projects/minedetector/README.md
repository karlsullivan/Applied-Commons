# MineDetector

**Status: incubation-only; no operational or field-ready capability claimed.**

MineDetector explores whether detection and mapping technologies could support humanitarian research on land mines and unexploded ordnance. The intended public benefit is better evidence about technical possibilities and limitations.

## Incubation scope

- Review prior work and identify research questions with qualified subject-matter experts.
- Document sensing limitations, potential failure modes, and validation needs.
- Consider simulations and controlled bench studies with inert, non-hazardous surrogates only.

Expected outputs at this stage are research notes, a bounded project brief, and proposed evaluation methods. Progress means clarifying a useful question and its evidence requirements; it does not imply readiness for deployment.

## Safety boundary

No work in suspected hazardous areas, handling of explosive ordnance, or operational clearance is in scope. Experimental results must not be used to declare land safe or guide safety decisions. Missed detections and misleading confidence are critical risks; surrogate tests cannot establish performance against real hazards.

Any proposed real-world use requires appropriate specialist validation and engagement with qualified humanitarian mine-action organisations. Required expertise includes humanitarian mine action, sensing, and statistical evaluation. Follow [SAFETY.md](../../SAFETY.md).
