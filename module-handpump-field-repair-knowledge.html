Self-contained repair knowledge base (schematics, part numbers, step-by-step repair guides, quality-control and injection-moulding know-how) for the Afridev and India Mark II handpumps, plus the MIT-licensed Afridev condition-monitoring firmware, so a locally trained technician can diagnose and restore a broken pump in the field - targeting ~90% water-point functionality from ~75% today.
