This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open hardware and software platform for autonomous UAVs (GPL-2.0): flight controller, autopilot and ground station, self-buildable and field-replaceable, with documented use on vehicles from 11.9 g to 25 kg. The open-source fixed-wing Paparazzi UAV carried the survey camera in the Kanderfirn alpine glacier monitoring (high mountain, low air pressure, poor GPS), demonstrating a low-cost, open aerial-mapping hardware path that operates in cold, harsh environments. Consumes nothing proprietary; pairs its photo output with the ODM photogrammetry station.
