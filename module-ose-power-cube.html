This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Self-contained engine-driven hydraulic power unit that powers several Global Village Construction Set machines through quick couplers: one shared 28 HP engine, fixed-displacement hydraulic pump (published rated point 3000 PSI / 15.2 GPM), 7-gallon fuel tank and a welded frame cage. It is a cross-domain mechanical power unit that complements the DC electrical modules by giving common-tooled mechanical power to fabrication and material-processing machines.
