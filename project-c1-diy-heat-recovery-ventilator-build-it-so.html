DIY Heat Recovery Ventilator (Build-It-Solar, incl. University of Saskatchewan design)
NeedAir and breathing
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestemperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Documentation page collecting DIY HRV designs for cold climates, including a 2-page DIY HRV developed at the University of Saskatchewan (Besant, Dumont, Van Ee) reproduced from W. Shurcliff's 'Air-To-Air Heat Exchangers for Houses'. Provides cold-climate (Canadian prairie origin) ventilation-with-heat-recovery design references buildable with common materials. Reference/documentation resource rather than a single turnkey design.
