A multi-circuit valve-actuator controller for radiant floor heating, running Tasmota on an ESP32 with relay/triac output for up to ten circuit valves. It holds per-zone room setpoints with PI control and ramp-up for large thermal-mass rooms, with frost protection on by default. No cloud, no subscription; it publishes valve position and zone temperature to the local MQTT bus and exposes Modbus for multi-zone orchestration from the home climate hub.
