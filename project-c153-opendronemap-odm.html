This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source (AGPL-3.0) command-line photogrammetry toolkit that turns simple drone/balloon/kite photos into georeferenced orthomosaics, DEMs, 3D models and point clouds; active and community-run since 2014 with desktop app and docs. Produces the open village-and-building-level basemaps and post-disaster exposure layers the brief needs, at low cost and fully self-hostable. Cold-climate proof: tested in the field on the Kanderfirn glacier (Bernese Alps, ~2300-3600 m) 2017/2018, achieving 0.7-1.2 m horizontal accuracy.
