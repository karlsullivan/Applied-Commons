This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source reusable N95-class respirator developed at Toronto General Hospital (Canada): 3D-printed frame + silicone casting + standard HME filters, designed for scale manufacture in small community/hospital facilities. Two peer-reviewed PLOS ONE papers (2020, 2021) cover development and subject validation. Investigational medical device, not regulator-approved; addresses the breathing-zone protection gap (smog/cooking PM) with a reusable, low-import-consumables design.
