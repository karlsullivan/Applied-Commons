This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source (GPL) crowdsourcing and mapping platform that helps communities turn citizen-generated information into action, used for humanitarian and disaster relief, crisis preparedness, good governance, human rights and climate resilience. Provides an open, self-hostable way to collect, map and share safety/hazard and crisis data for communities without reliable internet. Maturity: high (ushahidi/platform, 729 stars).
