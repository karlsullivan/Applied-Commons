LGPL-2.1 GPU/AD-friendly Julia library implementing soil freezing-characteristic and water-retention curves, with tools for inferring frozen-soil and permafrost state - supports carbon-storage modelling in freezing (cold-climate) soils. 8 stars, active (pushed 2026-11-02).
