Open (CC-BY-SA) CNC-fabricated structural-timber (18 mm ply/OSB) modular block system: clip-together carbon-negative chassis assembled on site in days by teams without conventional construction skills; regionally-grown timber, design-for-deconstruction/reuse, with a documented 'extremely cold climate' insulation path. A share-alike base design - keep CC-BY-SA and name it.
