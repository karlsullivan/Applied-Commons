Open Footwear

From Applied Commons, the open catalogue of ways to meet human needs
Open Footwear
NeedClothing and bodily protection
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestemperate
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Community of open-source shoe designs (running and barefoot) released under CC BY-SA 4.0, with downloadable files for digital manufacturing and repairable, customisable, community-modified shoes. Helps makers who want to build and repair their own footwear from open designs; active project (2026 Maker Faire, growing design library). Maturity: mature and documented; note designs target digital-fab (FabLab) tooling, and some are not purely hand-tool builds.
