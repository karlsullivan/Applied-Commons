This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Global community wiki of free, open (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0) step-by-step repair guides and teardowns for consumer electronics, plus a parts marketplace and multilingual translation (Crowdin). Free to share and adapt for noncommercial purposes. Maturity: very mature, large, multilingual, decade-plus old. Meets the electronics requirement for free open repair guides + spare-parts/repair information, meeting/exceeding the repair-information standard EU Directive 2024/1799 imposes on EU manufacturers from 31 July 2026. Climate-agnostic, so the know-how works year-round in cold climates (Canada, Scandinavia, Scotland, northern US, Alaska).
