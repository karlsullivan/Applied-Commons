Lightweight open-source (AGPL-3.0-or-later, Django + Leaflet, since 2015, 1,614 stars) tool that creates and embeds shareable OpenStreetMap-based maps in minutes from GeoJSON/GPX/KML/OSM layers, with POI drawing and heatmaps. Used for rapid community and emergency/disaster situational maps and dissemination on low-tech channels (shared links, embeds, print). Complements the heavier field-collection and photogrammetry modules as the last-mile 'show it to the community' layer, with independent adoption documented in humanitarian response.
