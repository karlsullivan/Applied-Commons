This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Government of Canada open (public domain) work on energy generation and storage in cold climates, including a pre-populated rapid-analysis tool for Canadian remote/off-grid communities (hourly load profiles, site wind & solar data, diesel-reduction and renewable-penetration results). Maturity: active government research centre, public domain. Works year-round in long-freezing-winter / low-winter-sun climates (Canada, Alaska, Scandinavia); addresses the energy requirement for applied know-how on maintaining electrification and power in harsh settings.
