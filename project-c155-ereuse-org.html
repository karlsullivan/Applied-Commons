This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source platform (tools + procedures + open data, organised as a common-pool resource) for the circular management of electronics: Workbench (self-booting device diagnostic/inventory), DeviceHub (IT asset management with QR traceability), and collection-point/traceability systems, explicitly to facilitate repair and reuse and address informal e-waste recycling in both developed and developing contexts. Meets the repair-first, local-recovery, no-export-logistics e-waste requirement. Mature and used by multiple organizations; eReuseIT is the flagship software.
