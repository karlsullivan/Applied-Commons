This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Locally-buildable, passive acoustic barrier (mass-loaded panel / mineral-wool infill in a local-wood or recycled-metal frame) that reduces transmitted night noise toward the <40 dB(A) Lnight outdoor and <30 dB(A) indoor bedroom targets for small shared housing, installed with hand tools and local materials. Passive, no powered components; the design and fabrication spec are fully open. Residual noise is confirmed with an ac-sound-meter or a phone-microphone reading.
