This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Offline XForm data capture on a ~US$20 feature phone for the 46% of the world without a smartphone. Form fill, local queue, and opportunistic OpenRosa sync (USB tether / local WiFi) to ODK Central or Kobo. Basis: the brief's constraint that only 54% own a smartphone and that a US$30 handset would unlock 1.6B people.
