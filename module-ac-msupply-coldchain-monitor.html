This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Continuous 2-8 C vaccine/medicine cold-chain monitoring for LMIC health facilities: BLE/USB temperature loggers (WHOPQS-approved sensor types, e.g. Berlinger), single and cumulative excursion alerts, sensor lifecycle and calibration management, cold-chain-equipment (CCE) functional-status tracking, scheduled preventative maintenance and 5-year capacity gap analysis (CCEI), and cold-chain inventory/issue tracking inside the mSupply LMIS. Serves requirement R3 directly and the monitoring facet of R1/R2. Documented field use in 30-40 LMICs including a DFAT-funded COVAX batch-level cold-chain deployment.
