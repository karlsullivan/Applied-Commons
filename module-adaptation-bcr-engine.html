This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open decision/appraisal layer that turns the other modules' outputs into the benefit-cost ratios the brief requires (documented BCR 2-10): probabilistic climate-impact and averted-damage calculation producing a per-measure cost, averted-damage benefit and benefit/cost ratio, with discounting via net present value and present/future scenarios - e.g. appraising a mangrove/marsh defence measure against grey works.
