NEW (non-commercial). Mobile poultry house: a wheeled / towable structure that moves livestock (poultry) onto fresh ground for daily feeding and manure distribution, with a predator-secure run, roof, feed/water points and an egg-collection area. Adds a low-input animal-protein source for the homestead / small-farm / village scenarios and closes the production-inputs gap (local protein) with basic skills.
