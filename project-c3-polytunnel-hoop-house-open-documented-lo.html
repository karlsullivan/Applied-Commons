Polytunnel / Hoop house - open, documented low-cost growing structure

Polytunnel / Hoop house - open, documented low-cost growing structure
NeedFood and nutrition
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestropical, arid, temperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

A low-cost hoop-house / polytunnel structure, documented as a public design (frame, covering, ventilation, passive solar heating). Provides season extension and lets cold-hardy crops overwinter; in colder regions such as Quebec, Canada, it extends the growing season by 2-3 months - a year-round cold-climate production option for fruits and vegetables through low-sun winter months. Helps small-scale growers extend local food availability; cheap, locally buildable with basic metalwork and framing skills, and operable without grid power. (Documented building technique/design; distinct from the already-known hydroponics projects.)
