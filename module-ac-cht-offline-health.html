This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Offline-first digital health apps for community health workers on low-end/feature-class devices: identify diseases, monitor pregnancies, follow up at-risk cases, and support remote clinical consultation where distance is the barrier. Data entry works over SMS as well as mobile apps; localization to any written language.
