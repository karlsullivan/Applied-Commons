This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Low-cost river-level, rainfall and atmospheric sensing node with local logging and threshold/rate-of-rise alert logic; the sensing half of the water-hazard early-warning requirement, covering floods, drought and emerging hazards such as glacial lake outburst floods that current systems do not handle.
