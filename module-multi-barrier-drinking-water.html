This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Closes the core need-brief gap: a single, no-power, locally buildable household package that removes turbidity and bacteria (biosand/slow-sand filtration), inactivates viruses and protozoa (a dedicated disinfection step that survives low-sun), and removes chemical contaminants (fluoride/arsenic/lead via a GAC/ion-exchange stage sized by the gac-ix toolkit), delivering >=15 L/person/day and <1 NTU / <1 CFU/100 mL-class water that the water-quality probe then verifies.
