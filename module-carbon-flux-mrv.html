This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open, auditable MRV layer for biological carbon removal: per-pixel (30 m) and per-zone gross GHG emissions, gross carbon removals (sequestration) and net flux across all carbon pools (aboveground, belowground, dead wood, litter, soil), covering mangroves and planted forests, 2001-2025, applying IPCC forest-inventory rules. This is the open verification layer the brief requires so carbon-removal claims are measured rather than assumed, closing the 'no low-cost open way to verify durable storage' gap.
