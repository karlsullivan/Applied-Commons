PMV/PPD and the adaptive model are the recognised, free-to-compute basis for setpoints, replacing vendor 'comfort' settings, and the adaptive branch fits non-air-conditioned low-income buildings. The WHO floor is added because in cold, low-income homes, under-heating harms health (respiratory and cardiovascular), which matters more than comfort. The rest are the verification's corrections: separating the methods, operative temperature, explicit inputs, applicability guards and local limits.

Specification

Health floor: in heated dwellings and facilities, operative temperature of at least 18 C in occupied rooms during the cold season (WHO Housing and Health Guidelines, 2018), higher where infants, the elderly or the sick are present. The floor comes before energy saving. Spaces with heating or mechanical cooling running: PMV -0.5 to +0.5 (PPD under 10 %) per ISO 7730 / EN 16798-1 category II, computed on operative temperature with explicit inputs: clothing 1.0 clo in winter and 0.5 clo in summer, metabolic rate 1.0-1.3 met. That gives about 20-24 C in winter and 23-26 C in summer. Relative humidity 30-60 % (higher encourages mould and dust mites). Naturally ventilated, occupant-controlled spaces with no mechanical cooling or heating running: the adaptive method of EN 16798-1 or ASHRAE 55, within its applicability range (running-mean outdoor temperature from 10 C up to 30 C in EN 16798-1, or 33.5 C in ASHRAE 55, over 7-30 days). A controller that cannot get the outdoor running mean uses the PMV band. Mixed-mode spaces use the PMV band whenever mechanical cooling or heating is running. Elevated air speed (ASHRAE 55): fans moving air at up to 0.8 m/s, or 1.2 m/s under occupant control, may extend the warm limit to about 30 C. This is separate from the adaptive method. Local discomfort limits per ISO 7730 category B: vertical air-temperature difference, floor temperature 19-29 C, radiant asymmetry and draught rate. Calculations use an open implementation (pythermalcomfort). Scope: design and control targets for heating, cooling and ventilation modules; not a connection interface.
