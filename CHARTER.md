# Applied Commons Charter

Applied Commons is a for-purpose engineering initiative building, testing, measuring, and publishing practical solutions to environmental, humanitarian, and public-interest problems.

## How we work

- Prefer practical engineering and reproducible evidence over advocacy or speculation.
- Produce experiments, designs, datasets, test results, reproducible systems, and documented failures.
- Publish methods, evidence, and limitations where possible; explain any safety, privacy, or third-party restrictions on publication.
- Measure real outcomes against stated success criteria, rather than meetings, publicity, or activity.
- Design for accessibility and replication, and involve people with the expertise needed to evaluate the work.
- Keep coordination lightweight: a clear project record, experiment records, and useful technical review.

## Current focus

[OpenCarbon](projects/opencarbon/README.md) is the first active technical stream. [MineDetector](projects/minedetector/README.md) is incubation-only; experimental detection work is not operational safety equipment.

## Public benefit and use

Work is intended for public-benefit and non-commercial use. Software follows [Licence.md](Licence.md); documentation and other non-software materials follow [Licence-Docs.md](Licence-Docs.md), unless otherwise stated. Commercial use requires separate permission. Identify any project-specific or third-party licences.

Use [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to contribute and [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md) to plan and report work responsibly.
