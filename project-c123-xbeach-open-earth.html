Open-source (LGPL) 2D numerical model (open-earth, TU Delft origin, 35 stars) that simulates hydrodynamic and morphodynamic processes and storm impacts on sandy coasts; ships with an xbeach-toolbox for pre/post-processing. Used to model how nature-based and grey coastal defences perform under storm surge and sea-level rise, supporting the brief's coastal-defence cost-vs-protection comparisons.
