This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source platform for community health workers (the 'CHT') providing care in hard-to-reach communities: identifying diseases, monitoring pregnancies and following up at-risk cases on low-end/feature devices, enabling remote clinical follow-up where distance is the barrier. As of 2025 supports 182,000+ health workers across 24 countries reaching an estimated 90 million people, with offline-capable low-end device support. Very mature and globally deployed; meets the health/telemedicine requirement.
