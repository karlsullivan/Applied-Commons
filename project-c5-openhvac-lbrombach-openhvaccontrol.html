This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Raspberry Pi multi-stage, multi-zone HVAC controller (GPL-3.0, 53 stars) that replaces commercial thermostats and drives damper motors for up to two stages of heat, two of cooling and three zones — an open, low-cost, locally maintainable temperature controller for existing HVAC equipment.
