This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

U.S. EPA open tools (Python + R/Shiny GUIs) for predicting the effectiveness of granular activated carbon and ion-exchange treatment units for contaminant removal; MIT licensed, actively maintained (last push 2026-04); a design/selection aid rather than a buildable device.
