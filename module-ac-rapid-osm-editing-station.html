This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Web-based OSM editing station running Rapid (ISC License), the extended iD editor fork maintained by Meta with AI-generated suggestion layers for roads, buildings, sidewalks, address points and trees plus an open-data catalog to validate, with per-object human review before upload (v2.5.6, active). Provides the AI-assisted, human-in-the-loop building/street extraction needed to close the ~170,000 unmapped-village gap at volunteer scale; multilingual and web-based, so it runs on ordinary tablets/phones. Consumes the task assignments from the Tasking Manager hub and the basemap imagery from the OAM node; its OSM output becomes the open basemap the brief requires.
