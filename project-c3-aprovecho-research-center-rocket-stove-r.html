NeedFood and nutrition
StatusScreened

This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source, publicly accessible biomass cookstove research and design with 30+ years of documented, iterative work. Rocket stove designs are used for cooking and, as a fixed 'rocket mass heater,' for space heating - so one design family supports clean cooking year-round and winter heating, covering cold-climate freezing winters. Helps households and institutions currently on polluting fuels; the design is low-cost and locally buildable from basic materials and metalwork/masonry skills, and it is a WHO-recognized compliant biomass-stove route. Maturity: mature, well-documented, deployed in many countries; Aprovecho explicitly describes its research as open source and accessible.
