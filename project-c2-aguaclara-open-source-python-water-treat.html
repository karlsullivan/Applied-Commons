An open-source MIT-licensed Python package (pip-installable, actively maintained - last pushed 2025, with CI, docs, DOI, and a Zenodo release) from AguaClara Cornell / AguaClara Reach for designing and doing research on community water-treatment plants. It provides DESIGN, MODELING, PLANNING, and ANALYSIS sub-packages: sizing coagulation (CDC), flocculators, sedimentors, and filters, plus process control/data acquisition. It is the open software half of the well-known AguaClara community water treatment program - a documented, low-cost, locally buildable, no-grid treatment design platform for households and small communities. (Software, not point-of-use hardware.)
