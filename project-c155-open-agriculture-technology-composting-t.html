NeedMaterials and circularity
StatusDiscovered
LicenceNone found
This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open design library (Open Agriculture Technology Collective) for off-grid, zero-water composting toilet systems - bucket-system sizing, cover material, thermophilic decomposition and curing timelines, pathogen die-off data, and legal/regulatory guidance - turning sanitation waste into soil fertility. Helps off-grid/remote users design their own dry sanitation with local materials and no grid. Maturity: open, continuously updated design-guidance resource (not a single fabricated product).
