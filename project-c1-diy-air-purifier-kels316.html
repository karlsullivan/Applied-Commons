This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

A small, low-cost DIY air purifier: HEPA H13 vacuum-cleaner filter plus a 120mm PC fan in a 3D-printed housing, powered from a USB power bank; under AUD $100 in ~1 hour (excl. print time), with STL files, a full BOM and smoke-generator test notes. Originated for bushfire smoke (PM2.5). ~657 stars (2020).

References

  1. Wikipedia: 2019-20 Australian bushfire season (Black Summer) - 417 indirect smoke deaths, 24M ha burned, 11,774+ fires, $100B damage (need context) en.wikipedia.org
