Open Home Assistant/HACS integration that schedules multi-zone irrigation with weather adjustment, sun-event/crontab timing, eco-mode volume limits, and auto-shutoff - cutting garden/crop/homestead water waste; runs on a local HA server (no cloud, no internet), so it satisfies the no-connectivity constraint; reuses existing valve hardware.
