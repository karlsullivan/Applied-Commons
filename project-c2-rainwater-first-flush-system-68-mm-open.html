Open, freely downloadable 3D-print model (Parametric Model Maker) for a first-flush diverter that keeps stored rainwater cleaner: the first dirty runoff fills a vertical chamber until a floating ball seals it, then clean rain flows to the water butt/tank. Runs entirely on gravity and a float ball - no power required - and is documented as suitable for water butts, rain barrels, garden irrigation, greenhouses, and off-grid water systems. Works in rainy/low-sun periods and in cold-climate rainwater harvesting where the collection season is short; it is the collection side of the path and is locally printable.
