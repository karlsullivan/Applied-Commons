This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source (AGPL-3.0) Android app to track and verify reforestation globally; captures individual planting locations plus tree growth and survival in the field, directly addressing the brief's gap that only ~5% of planting organisations report survival rates. Works offline, so usable at planting sites in cold climates year-round. 102 stars, active (pushed 2026-11).
