Open-source at-home vertical farm (hydroponics unit) from Hectar Agriculture, inspired by open-source electronics and 3D-printing projects. The full design package is published free - manual, documentation and CAD (GrabCAD), with large files via torrent - so a DIYer can build their own stacked indoor vertical farm to grow fresh food at home. Designed specifically for interiors. Maturity: mid-stage - public v0.9 design package (2021), small core team and community-driven.
