4-20 mA is the universal, cheap and robust way to connect field instruments: it carries power and signal on two wires, survives long cable runs and electrical noise, and its live zero makes a broken wire visible. Almost every industrial transmitter supports it, new or second-hand. M12 connectors are the sealed, standard sensor connector. Together with ac-modbus-control, this covers instrumentation and control.

Specification

Analogue field instruments (level, pressure, flow and temperature transmitters) connect to controllers with a 4-20 mA current loop per IEC 60381-1, powered from 24 V DC (the 24 V bus of ac-dc-bus-elv, or a 24 V supply), two-wire loop-powered where the instrument allows. 4 mA is the bottom of the range and 20 mA the top. Failure signals per NAMUR NE 43: at or below 3.6 mA, or at or above 21 mA, is a fault, never a measurement, and controllers treat it as one (alarm and safe action). Each instrument's documentation gives its range in engineering units, its loop voltage drop and its failure direction. Receivers use a sense resistor of 250 ohm (1-5 V) or less and leave enough loop voltage for the instrument at 21 mA. Connector: M12 A-coded, 4-pin, per IEC 61076-2-101. Two-wire instruments: pin 1 loop supply (+24 V), pin 2 loop return to the receiver. Three- and four-wire instruments: pin 1 +24 V, pin 3 0 V, pin 2 current output, pin 4 spare or a switching output. Discrete on/off sensors and switches use the same connector at 24 V DC. Cable: shielded twisted pair with the shield earthed at the controller end only; runs outdoors or over about 30 m get surge protection at the controller. HART on the loop is optional. Instruments in an explosive atmosphere (for example the gas space of a biogas digester) must be certified for that zone under IEC 60079; ordinary instruments must not be used there.
