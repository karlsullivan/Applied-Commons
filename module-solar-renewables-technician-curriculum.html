Open, regionally scalable applied-technical curriculum to grow trained solar and renewable-energy technician output (~40% by 2030 target): a free 10-lesson course (energy efficiency, solar thermal, solar electric, wind, micro-hydro, renewable energy in the developing world, and renewable-energy economics) plus K-12 and teacher-training modules.
