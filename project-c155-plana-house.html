This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Freely downloadable 42 m2 two-bedroom compact timber home by Swiss studio Plana Architecture (Kristijan Markoc), designed to UK and Irish (cold-climate) construction regulations from off-the-shelf materials available in any local hardware store; pitched-roof single-storey on screw piles/pad foundations, cladding finished with locally available materials. Markoc makes a complete architectural drawing set free so people can 'understand, adapt and potentially build their own house'; a prototype is planned. COLD-CLIMATE: UK/Ireland regulatory context, simple timber frame. Young/new (released 2026) but full open drawings.
