This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Simple, locally repairable controller that runs a water pump from a PV + battery (DC bus) system based on battery voltage, sunlight level and tank max-level. Fills the household plot tank or community tank in the dry-season peak when the grid is absent and diesel fuel costs dominate the pumping life-cycle cost. Serves the water requirement (R3) and the irrigation requirement (R4) for a single-borehole smallholder plot. Reuses the existing corpus code solar-pump-controller.
