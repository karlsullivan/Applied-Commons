This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open (AGPL-3.0) self-hostable common-pool platform for circular electronics: Workbench (self-booting USB/PXE device diagnostic + inventory, immutable hardware snapshot, QR traceability), DeviceHub (self-hostable IT asset management, runs on 2 cores/2 GB RAM), and procedures + open data. Removes the software/traceability/accountability barrier that stops local refurbishment operations from forming and scaling; repair-first, local pathways, no export dependency, informal-sector inclusion.
