This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Locally built, no-power filtration stage for a household: a sand bed that cuts turbidity to <=5 NTU (ideally <1 NTU) and removes >3.5-log E. coli/bacteria; the physical first barrier of the multi-barrier package, feeding the disinfection stage and storage.
