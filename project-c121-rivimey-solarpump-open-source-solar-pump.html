An open-source (GitHub) solar-powered water-pump controller with a battery for reserve power, built to pump water from a stream for watering an allotment/garden and designed to be fully portable. It addresses the water and food/income (dry-season irrigation of a small plot) requirements at smallholder scale with a low-cost, battery-buffered PV pump that keeps delivering water at the peak (dry season) when the grid is absent, and its open design is maintainable locally with common tools.
