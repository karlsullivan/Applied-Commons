Peer-reviewed, open-source Python toolkit for steady-state thermal-plant simulation with a credible, validated vapor-compression refrigeration capability: CoolProp fluid properties (incl. low-GWP and natural refrigerants such as R744/CO2), exergy analysis, part-load operation, pymoo optimization, and a documented CO2 transcritical booster refrigeration model library (supermarket-scale, COP ~4.7 at 15 C ambient, ELFORSK-funded study). Provides the open, right-sized design layer for cold rooms and absorption/compression systems, replacing hand/textbook sizing or expensive closed simulators (EBSILON, Aspen Plus). Serves the design path of R1 and R2.
