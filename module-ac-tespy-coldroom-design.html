Peer-reviewed, open-source Python toolkit for steady-state thermal-plant simulation with a credible, validated vapor-compression refrigeration capability: CoolProp fluid properties (incl. low-GWP and natural refrigerants such as R744/CO2) to size compressors, condensers and evaporators for the solar-cold-room and last-mile boxes. The design-time tool that makes the hardware tiers buildable without a fab-lab engineer.
