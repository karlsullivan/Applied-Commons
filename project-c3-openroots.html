This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source design for autonomous hydroponics with sensors (pH, EC, temperature, VPD, CO2) and relay control for nutrient dosing, lighting, and climate, aimed at affordable, low-water local food production in small spaces. Helps hobbyists and community growers. Maturity: early - explicitly described as in its infancy with beta testing planned.
