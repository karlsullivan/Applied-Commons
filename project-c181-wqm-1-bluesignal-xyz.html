This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source continuous water-quality monitor on a Raspberry Pi Zero 2W measuring pH, TDS, turbidity, ORP, EC/conductivity and temperature, with LoRaWAN + GPS, a six-channel PCB sensor board, and 4 optoisolated relays to drive dosing pumps, aerators and valves. Open hardware (CERN-OHL-S-2.0) and open firmware (GPL-3.0), designed for local buildability and no proprietary cloud; new (2026, no stars yet) but directly addresses the low-cost field water-quality, off-grid and actuation requirements.
