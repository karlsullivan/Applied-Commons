This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

The smallest viable room thermostat: one DHT22 (temperature + humidity) and one relay on an Arduino Nano, running climaduino firmware. It holds a fixed temperature setpoint with ~2 F on/off hysteresis, adds humidity overcooling and an away humidity-only mode, short-cycle protection and EEPROM persistence of settings. The cheapest per-room device for damp climates and non-central-AC spaces the hub does not reach.
