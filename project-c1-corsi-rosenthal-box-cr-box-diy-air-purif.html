Corsi-Rosenthal Box (CR Box) DIY air purifier

Corsi-Rosenthal Box (CR Box) DIY air purifier
NeedAir and breathing
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestropical, arid, temperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

The well-documented DIY HEPA/MERV room purifier: a box, a box fan and stacked furnace (MERV-13) filters, ~US$75 build. UC Davis testing shows 165-239 CFM CADR at that cost (one build reached ~850 CFM); documented trade-offs: MERV-13 is ~85% efficient on virus-sized particles per pass (vs >99.95% HEPA), can be noisy (up to 67 dBa on high), and is the least energy-efficient of 23 tested purifiers (2.17-2.19 CADR/W). Needs a power source and a filter supply chain, so it covers room-air cleaning for households with basic power rather than the no-power hearth gap.
