StatusApproved
ApprovedNovember 2026
Used by1 module

Three modules in this set (handpump-technician-training, solar-renewables-technician-curriculum, medical-device-om-training) all certify technicians across water, energy and health and must share 'who is competent for what' with the wider system to place and verify staff. No approved or pending standard covers this cross-domain competency/certification record, and it is not a single module's own report - it is consumed by the system to route work. It is not in the rejected list.

Specification

A technician's competency and certification record is exchanged as a W3C Verifiable Credential (Verifiable Credentials Data Model v2.0, W3C Recommendation 2025), serialised as UTF-8 JSON (RFC 8259 / ISO/IEC 21778:2017) with ISO 8601-1 UTC timestamps. Mapping: issuer = the certifying body or training provider, with a stable issuer identifier; credentialSubject = the technician, identified by a pseudonymous stable identifier where feasible, with the per-skill competency results (pass/fail), the certification level and the field-work log as claims; evidence = the completed training units and assessments; validFrom and validUntil carry issue and expiry. Certification level and expiry are anchored to a named certification scheme (in its credentialSchema or claims), ideally operated by a body accredited to ISO/IEC 17024, whose rules set the validity period, renewal and re-assessment; a record without a named scheme is a training record, not a certification. Tamper evidence: every record carries an issuer proof (a Data Integrity proof or a JOSE/COSE-secured form as the VC 2.0 family defines), checked by a verifier before the record is relied on to route work. Data protection (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679): only the fields needed for placing and verifying staff are collected; the lawful basis and the purpose are stated; the field-work log and personal data follow a stated retention and deletion schedule; special-category (health) data that the medical-device module may touch is kept out of the credential. Records management per ISO 15489-1:2016: creation, authenticity, custody and retention metadata are kept for each certification and field-work record. For the medical-device-om-training module, the fields map to ISO 13485:2016 clause 6.2 (competence) and its document-control requirements. Within the EU, the record can also be issued as an electronic attestation under the European Digital Identity Framework (Regulation (EU) 2024/1183).
