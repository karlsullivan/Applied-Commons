This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Global volunteer network holding 4,000+ community repair events (the 4,000th opened in Canada), where trained volunteers repair household electronics and other goods, sharing free open repair know-how and skills. Runs year-round indoors in cold-climate countries (Canada, EU), and extends right-to-repair-style practical repair knowledge. Mature, well-documented, local and low-cost.
