This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open Source Ecology's open-design wind turbine (a GVCS 'Energy' machine), developed under Creative Commons by OSE, headquartered at the Factor e Farm in Maysville, Missouri (US), with an independent OSE Germany effort - both long-winter, low-winter-sun climates - and research concentrated on a vertical-axis wind turbine testbed (TiVA) with full build documentation on the OSE wiki. Provides locally buildable, commonly-tooled generation that can complement solar for lighting, charging and pumping in remote settlements that will not be grid-reached for years, supporting the brief's stand-alone and local-O&M constraints and the cold-climate requirement.
