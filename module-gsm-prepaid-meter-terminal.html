This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

OEM-agnostic GSM prepaid meter that measures energy (or water) and unlocks it against prepaid credit, working offline with no subscription. Covers the need brief's offline prepaid metering (water, eWATERpay ~US$0.01/20 L) and PAYGo installment metering for the 733M off-grid, and is the per-meter primitive the token backend and core-banking module settle against.
