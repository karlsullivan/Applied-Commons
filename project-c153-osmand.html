This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Free and open-source offline maps and navigation app (Android/iOS) on OpenStreetMap data: download region maps to the device, then route (car/bike/foot), search, record GPX tracks and navigate with visual and voice guidance with zero connectivity; privacy-first, no account or location tracking. COLD-CLIMATE (works year-round in cold regions): it is fully offline, and its North America maps — the United States (including Alaska), Canada and Mexico — are built from high-resolution national elevation (DEM) data, giving detailed terrain where winter/low-sun navigation is hardest. Who it helps: hikers, cyclists, drivers, humanitarian responders and communities needing reliable offline maps in remote or low-connectivity areas. Maturity: since ~2010, GPLv3 (code), ~6.1k GitHub stars, very active (Nov 2026), free plus a Plus tier.
