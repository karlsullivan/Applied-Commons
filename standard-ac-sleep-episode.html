Sleep episode and circadian-rhythm record

StatusApproved
ApprovedNovember 2026
Used by6 modules

The AASM Scoring Manual (Version 3, 2023) is the clinical reference for stage coding; DLMO and the time of the core temperature minimum are the standard circadian phase markers, each a time point defined by its own method; ISO 8601/RFC 3339 is already a house standard. Open and free; makes sleep records clinically comparable instead of device-specific.

Specification

One JSON record per scored episode: subject_id (pseudonym); recording_start and recording_end (ISO 8601 / RFC 3339 UTC with device offset, per ac-iso8601-timestamps); epoch_duration (s, default 30 s); a per-epoch stage array coded to the current AASM Manual for the Scoring of Sleep and Associated Events (Version 3, February 2023: W, N1, N2, N3, R), with the manual version recorded in the record and a required crosswalk field that maps any proprietary or actigraphy-derived scale to the AASM stage. Circadian phase markers, both optional: DLMO (dim-light melatonin onset) as a time point - an RFC 3339 timestamp, or an offset in minutes from a stated reference (sleep onset or lights on) - with the assay (salivary or plasma melatonin, sampled in dim light) and the onset threshold used (e.g. 4 pg/ml in saliva); and Tmin (core body temperature minimum) as the time of the minimum, with the minimum temperature in degC as an optional separate attribute and the method (rectal probe or ingestible sensor). A wrist (distal skin) temperature estimate is a proxy, not core temperature: it goes in the derived-estimate fields, flagged as an estimate. Actigraphy-derived circadian estimates are not DLMO: they go in the actigraphy fields, flagged as estimates, with the pyActigraphy-compatible actigraphy-epoch flag. The previously ambiguous 'RAMC' field is removed and replaced by the AASM stage array.
