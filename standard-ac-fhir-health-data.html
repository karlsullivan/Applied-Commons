The open (CC0) standard for health data exchange. OpenSRP is FHIR-native and OpenMRS bridges to it, so health-worker registers, clinic records and device telemetry interoperate. Pinning R4 4.0.1 with the International Patient Summary and WHO SMART Guidelines profiles, which are built for low- and middle-income health systems, makes interoperability testable; the verification's example profile, US Core, is US-specific. Privacy controls are included because health records are the most sensitive data the modular set holds.

Specification

Health data exchange uses HL7 FHIR R4 (v4.0.1), the release used by the International Patient Summary and WHO SMART Guidelines implementation guides and by OpenSRP 2; moving to R5 waits until those guides move. Profiles: patient summaries to the HL7 FHIR International Patient Summary IG 2.0.1 (STU 2, designed to EN 17269 and ISO 27269), and in the EU also to the European electronic health record exchange format for patient summaries once it is adopted under the European Health Data Space Regulation ((EU) 2025/327). Maternal, child-health and immunisation records follow the matching WHO SMART Guidelines IGs (antenatal care, immunizations) where one exists; these are still pre-release (versions 0.x), so a module pins the exact IG version it implements and expects changes. Otherwise base R4 resources. Device readings are Observation linked to Device, vital signs per the FHIR vital-signs profile with LOINC codes and UCUM units. Exchange: FHIR REST API or Bundles in JSON. Each system publishes a CapabilityStatement, and exported Bundles are validated against the profiles they claim (for example with the HL7 FHIR validator) before they are accepted. Privacy and security: health records are special-category personal data (GDPR Art. 9 in the EU). Access is only for authenticated users with roles (SMART App Launch / OAuth 2.0 where the system supports it); TLS 1.2 or later in transit; encryption at rest on portable devices; consent recorded (Consent) and access audited (AuditEvent). Licence: the FHIR specification is CC0. Scope: data exchange only. It does not approve any medical device: incubators and other medical electrical equipment fall under IEC 60601-1 and medical device regulation.
