This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

GPLv3 open-source, embedded CNC g-code controller that runs on a plain Arduino, turning inexpensive stepper motors and a simple frame into a functioning CNC mill/router for cutting, forming and joining wood and metal. It is the enabling firmware for a large ecosystem of DIY and open CNC machines. Mains-powered and indoor, hence usable year-round in cold climates. Very mature (first released 2009, v1.1, actively maintained), low-cost, locally buildable, with extensive documentation.
