This project is under research: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source mobile-messaging platform (originally UNICEF RapidSMS 2007, open-sourced with TextIt 2014; Digital Public Good 2020) for designing and scaling interactive apps over SMS, WhatsApp and voice/IVR on basic phones. Powers governments and development partners: 130 UNICEF country offices, 284 active workspaces, 5+ billion messages; used for early warning, health programs and market/advisory flows. Very mature, documented, localizable to many languages; SMS/IVR makes it work on feature phones and offline-friendly. Strong fit for the brief's SMS/IVR and localization requirements.
