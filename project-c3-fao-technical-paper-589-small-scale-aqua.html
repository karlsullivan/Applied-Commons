This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

FAO Fisheries & Aquaculture Technical Paper No. 589 (Somerville et al., 2014, 288 pp, ISBN 978-92-5-108532-5), open access. Authoritative small-scale design/sizing/parts reference: nitrogen cycle and unit balancing, water-quality parameters and sourcing, unit design across the three methods (media beds, NFT, deep water culture), fish/plant/bacteria management and troubleshooting, local/sustainable inputs, and nine appendices including tools to size biofilter media and ammonia load for a given stocking density/feed, homemade fish-feed production, and a cost-benefit analysis of a small-scale media-bed unit. Best used as the sizing + costing backbone. (Retrieved abstract does not explicitly address cold-climate operation.)
