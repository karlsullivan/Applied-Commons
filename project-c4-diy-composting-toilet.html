This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

A DIY composting toilet (built to save water) that publishes 3D models of a urine adapter (5/8in vinyl tube to bottle) and a urine diverter, with an assembly document in progress. Open hardware, low-cost, locally printable; early/mid-stage.
