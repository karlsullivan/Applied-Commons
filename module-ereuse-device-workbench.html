Open (AGPL-3.0) self-booting USB/PXE device diagnostic and inventory kit (eReuse Workbench image): boots any PC from USB, produces an immutable hardware snapshot, applies QR traceability, and grades the device for repair/redistribution/recycling - the hardware+software entry point that any local refurbishing or school/municipal e-waste desk can deploy with no cloud and no EPR/municipal infrastructure.
