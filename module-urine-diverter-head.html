Urine-Diverting Dry Toilet Head

From Applied Commons, the open catalogue of ways to meet human needs
Urine-Diverting Dry Toilet Head
Kindhardware
Maturitydocumented
NeedsSanitation and hygiene
This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

A toilet seat/head that separates urine from faeces at the source (dry, waterless). Urine is diverted to a sealed canister; dry solids drop to a bin beneath the seat, where they receive a carbon additive (sawdust) to stay hygienic and odour-free. The core enabler for the compost-toilet cabin and the marine compact unit; a reusable head that can be dropped into any ac-dry-solid-seat-mount.
