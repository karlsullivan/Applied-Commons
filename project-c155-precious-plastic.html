This project is under research: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Global open-source (all content CC-BY) initiative that provides free blueprints and know-how so individuals and communities build their own small-scale plastic-recycling machines (shredder, extruder, injection molder, sheetpress) plus education/training videos and a global community platform, explicitly to recycle plastic locally instead of relying on large, inefficient plants. Meets 'local sourcing', 'no municipal infrastructure', and 'informal-sector workers' constraints. Mature (long-running, one of the largest open hardware communities).
