MIT-licensed open-source (Raspberry Pi based) multi-zone hydroponic/aquaponic/irrigation and fish-tank water management & monitoring platform - a software/automation layer that pairs with any build to track and control water quality, zones and pumps. GitHub: 306 stars / 31 forks, JavaScript, created 2020, last pushed 2024-09. Maturity: community-tracked open code; software layer only (not a build plan).
