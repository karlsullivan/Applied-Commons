NeedCold chain and storage
StatusDiscovered

This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Free public design handbook plus an Excel design toolbox (Efficiency for Access-funded) for locally building solar cold rooms. A 'how to build it' enabler that lowers the design and local-engineering barrier toward a ~$10k-class local-materials unit. Published public resource, not a turnkey product.
