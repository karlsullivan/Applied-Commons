This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source instrumentation for electricity, solar, storage and heat pumps built on Raspberry Pi and Arduino: emonPi3 (6-channel AC energy monitor with integrated Pi), emonTH3 (battery-powered wireless room/node sensor), and EmonCMS for full local or remote data logging and visualisation. Developed in Scotland (long freezing winters, low winter sun); battery-powered wireless nodes and full local logging are designed for off-grid, year-round operation in cold climates. Covers grid/energy health measurement and the open-data/calibration requirement.
