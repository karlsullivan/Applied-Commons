UAF Nanook EV - Design of an affordable electric snowmobile
NeedTransport and logistics
ServesMobility and independent living, Economic and livelihood security, Continuity of essential services
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatescold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Documented adult-scale open cold-climate vehicle design: the University of Alaska Fairbanks (UAF) Nanook EV team's design paper for the SAE Clean Snowmobile Challenge, retrieved in full (13 pages). Verified figures: 51.5 km range at 32 km/h under optimal snow conditions; drawbar pull >2.64 kN; BMS built on Peter Perkins' open-source BMS work; motor controller designed from the Open ReVolt plans; chassis designed for parts removable/replaced with a 10mm nut driver. A design-stage competition paper, not a maintained codebase/BOM, and not cargo-scale - but the only adult-scale open cold-climate vehicle design surfaced by the search.
