UC Davis chimney solar dryer
NeedFood and nutrition
StatusDiscovered
LicenceNone found
This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

A passive solar food dryer: air warmed in a solar collector rises through a chimney and over trays of produce. UC Davis publishes a construction and use manual. Production library design audit AUD01 (crop drying): acquire the build and use files, record rights and revision, select a crop and climate, and test airflow, product quality and storage.
