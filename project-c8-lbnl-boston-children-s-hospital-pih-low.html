LBNL / Boston Children's Hospital / PIH low-cost re-usable electricity-free (passive PCM) infant warmer

LBNL / Boston Children's Hospital / PIH low-cost re-usable electricity-free (passive PCM) infant warmer
NeedReproductive and early-life needs
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestropical, arid, temperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Inexpensive, re-usable, non-electric infant warmer documented in an open-access paper, used to complement kangaroo mother care (KMC) or as the sole external heat source. Fully offline/passive (no battery or mains). In a prospective study in rural Rwandan hospitals (33 infants, 102 encounters, up to 6 h), it corrected hypothermia in 95% of hypothermic encounters (41/43) and prevented hypothermia in 100% of at-risk encounters (59/59), maintaining goal temperature for the study duration in >=85% of uses. Directly fits the power-free neonatal thermal-care requirement; being passive it works year-round in cold climates where electronic warmers are scarce or fail.
