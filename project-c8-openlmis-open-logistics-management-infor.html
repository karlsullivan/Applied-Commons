Mature open-source system for managing medical commodity distribution (procurement, ordering, stock, expiry, supply-chain) in low- and middle-income countries. The maintained line is the v3+ Reference Distribution (openlmis-ref-distro), a Docker Compose deployment of published OpenLMIS service images plus reference UI modules; the earlier v2 'open-lmis' core repo is now archived. AGPL-3.0, long-lived (2012-), actively developed, widely deployed with Janssen/UNICEF and national supply chains. Directly addresses the reproductive-choice gap by managing the supply/distribution of modern-contraception and family-planning commodities (and other health commodities), supporting reduction of unmet need at scale. Software-only, so climate-agnostic and usable year-round in cold climates.
