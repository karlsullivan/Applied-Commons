This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Clinic-owned, open-source temperature-monitoring system for vaccine and medical refrigerators (records and alarms excursions) - the exact monitoring/calibration layer the brief identifies as the cheap, high-value health gap. Early-stage / exploratory (project announced May 2026 and recruiting contributors), so maturity is prototype-to-early rather than deployed.

References

  1. WHO Immunization Handbook for Medical Officers, Unit 4: Cold chain and logistics management (ILR +2..+8C, holdover at 43C for 20 hrs minimum, twice-daily logbook monitoring, real-time monitoring device with SMS alerts) cdn.who.int
  2. Cold chain - Wikipedia (vaccines can become inert due to lack of temperature control; vaccine cold-chain integrity is a critical public health concern) en.wikipedia.org
