This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Clinic-owned, open-source temperature-monitoring system for vaccine and medical refrigerators (records and alarms excursions) - the exact monitoring/calibration layer the brief identifies as the cheap, high-value health gap. Early-stage / exploratory (project announced May 2026 and recruiting contributors), so maturity is prototype-to-early rather than deployed.
