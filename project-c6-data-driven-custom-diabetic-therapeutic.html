Data-Driven Custom Diabetic Therapeutic Footwear (PLOS ONE)

From Applied Commons, the open catalogue of ways to meet human needs
Data-Driven Custom Diabetic Therapeutic Footwear (PLOS ONE)
NeedClothing and bodily protection
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestropical, temperate
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-access, peer-reviewed study (Zwaferink/Bus et al.) defining and validating evidence-based, custom-made therapeutic footwear and insoles that off-load metatarsal-head plantar pressure to prevent diabetic foot ulceration, including a documented CADCAM insole design/optimisation routine. Helps the ~830 million living with diabetes who lack affordable therapeutic footwear; the evidence-based conditions are handmade (no 3D-print dependency). Maturity: published and validated in 24 high-risk patients (17-53% metatarsal peak-pressure relief); a design protocol rather than a ready-to-copy consumer build.
