goscommons Biogas
NeedSanitation and hygiene
StatusUnder research
LicenceGPL-3.0
Sourcegithub.com
Last updated upstreamJuly 2024
This project is under research: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

A validated mesophilic plug-flow biodigester design (built ~2018 for ~US$125 in Cuba) sized 300x100x100 cm for family farms in tropical/high-temperature areas. Processes human and animal waste into cooking biogas plus organic fertilizer; includes photos, design specs and a fabrication document. Mature in concept, low-cost, locally buildable; early-stage repository.
