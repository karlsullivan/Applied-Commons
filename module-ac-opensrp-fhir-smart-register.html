FHIR-native offline smart-register for community health workers to register and follow pregnancies (ANC/PNC), children, immunizations and family planning in low-resource settings. FHIR Core (OpenSRP 2) is a Kotlin Android app that works offline-first (store-and-forward) with WHO standard diagnostic criteria, SDC clinical forms, encrypted data at rest and TLS in transit; the OpenSRP Server (HAPI FHIR + Keycloak) syncs across the team. Apache-2.0; countries adapt the reference apps (e.g. the WHO-ANC reference app) to local guidelines.
