Straw Bale Building (Chris Magwood & Peter Mack, open-access guide)
NeedMaterials and circularity
StatusDiscovered

This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-access PDF guide to planning, designing and building with straw bales - a local, low-embodied-carbon, carbon-sequestering wall material with superior insulation; the book's stated scope includes 'special concerns for northern climates.' Helps rural/remote builders in cold climates achieve energy-efficient, low-cost shelter from locally abundant material. Open and established: 2000 guide freely downloadable (retrieval of the PDF itself timed out on the extractor, so the cold-climate scope is cited from the guide's published description as quoted in the retrieved search index).
