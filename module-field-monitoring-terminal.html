This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open, locally buildable hardware+software that closes the monitoring gap (only ~5% of tree-planting organisations publish survival rates; sapling survival can fall to ~52%): a solar-powered, rugged, GPS + cellular/LoRa field terminal for geotagged tree/plot/plot-survival and environmental observations. Software stack follows the OpenTreeMap (otm-core) crowdsourced-inventory model so field data is published and verifiable rather than assumed.
