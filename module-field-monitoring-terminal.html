This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

Open, locally buildable hardware+software that closes the monitoring gap (only 5% of tree-planting organisations publish survival rates; sapling survival can fall to ~52%): a solar-powered, rugged, GPS + cellular/LoRa field terminal with a data-collection app derived from the OpenTreeMap observation model that records per-plot/per-tree survival, growth, species, location, date and photo evidence, and publishes survival-rate monitoring so restoration outcomes are verifiable rather than assumed.
