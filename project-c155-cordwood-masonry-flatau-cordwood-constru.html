Cordwood masonry (Flatau / Cordwood Construction Resources)
NeedMaterials and circularity
StatusDiscovered
LicenceNone found
This project is discovered: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Owner-builder shelter system using short lengths of waste/cut cordwood as mortar-filled infill with local materials; a documented cold-climate technique (decades of practice in northern Wisconsin, plus examples in Nova Scotia/Canada), using low-embodied-carbon wood, earth, and recycled glass. Helps DIY/low-income builders build warm, mortgage-free homes from local and waste wood. Mature and open: 40+ years of practice, free book 'Cordwood Construction: Best Practices' (Richard Flatau) openly downloadable, plus cordwoodconstruction.org guidance.
