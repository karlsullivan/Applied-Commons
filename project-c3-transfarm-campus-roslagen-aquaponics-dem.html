TransFarm - Campus Roslagen aquaponics demonstration (Norrtalje, Sweden)

This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

EU Interreg Central Baltic project (2023-2026, coordinated by Univ. of Turku) building year-round indoor aquaponics demos in Sweden, Estonia, Latvia. Public short construction report documents the small-scale Sweden system: ebb/flood media beds, 1 m3 DWC, sump with biofilter, 160 cm prefabricated fish tank, materials (wood/plywood/EPDM liner/PP/PVC), flood-risk mitigation, and a 12 V deep-cycle battery backup that runs the pumps and air pump for days through a power outage. Cold-climate (Scandinavia), documented open educational material; system built 2023.
