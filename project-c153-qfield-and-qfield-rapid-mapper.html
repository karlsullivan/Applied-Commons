This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Open-source mobile field-GIS app (Android/iOS/Windows/macOS/Linux) that runs QGIS projects offline in the field with high-precision GPS and local storage that syncs via cable or QFieldCloud; no persistent connection or specialist GIS staff required. QField Rapid Mapper is the open project layer that lets emergency responders, civil-protection teams, military and citizens quickly assess and report natural-disaster damage, meeting the offline, phone-first and real-time post-disaster requirements.
