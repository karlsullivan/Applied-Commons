PNNL / DOE 3D-Printed Mask Frames and Reusable Respirator Designs

PNNL / DOE 3D-Printed Mask Frames and Reusable Respirator Designs
NeedAir and breathing
StatusScreened
LicenceNone found
Climatestropical, arid, temperate, cold
This project is screened: Applied Commons has catalogued it but has not written a build pack for it.

Free downloadable custom-fit 3D-printed (or vacuum-formed/injection-molded) mask frames and reusable respirator designs from Pacific Northwest National Laboratory with four other DOE labs. Custom-fit frames cut air gaps and use less N95 filter material per mask, so a limited filter supply goes further - directly relevant to the 'no steady mask supply' constraint. Free STEP/SLDPRT files in six sizes.
