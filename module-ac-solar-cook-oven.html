This is a draft module. It has not been built or tested.

REUSE of existing module. Fuel-free solar thermal cooker (documented box, panel, parabolic and vacuum-tube designs from solarcooker.org) for household/homestead/small-farm clean cooking. No fuel, no combustion; clean at the point of use; operable by a non-specialist with basic skills. Closes the need brief's clean-cooking gap.
